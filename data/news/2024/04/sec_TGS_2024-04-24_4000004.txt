<P align=center style='font:11pt Calibri;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt;color:#000000'><B>TGS Files 2023 Annual Report on Form 20-F</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Calibri;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt;color:#000000'><B>Buenos Aires, Argentina, April 24, 2024</B>. &#160;Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (&#8220;tgs&#8221; or &#8220;the Company&#8221;) (NYSE: TGS, MERVAL: TGSU2) announced that today it filed its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023 (the &#8220;2023 Annual Report&#8221;) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the &#8220;SEC&#8221;). The 2023 Annual Report can be accessed by visiting the Company&#8217;s website at www.tgs.com.ar as well as on the SEC&#8217;s website at www.sec.gov. In addition, shareholders may receive a hard copy of the Company&#8217;s complete financial statements free of charge by requesting a copy from Carlos Almagro (<FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #000000'>calmagro@tgs.com.ar)</FONT> or Leandro Perez Casta&#241;o (<FONT style='color:#0000FF;border-bottom:1px solid #0000FF'>leandro_perez@tgs.com.ar</FONT>) in TGS&#8217;s Investor Relations Office at +(54-11) 4865-9050.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Calibri;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:8pt Calibri;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:0pt;text-indent:-21.25pt;color:#000000'><B><I> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;tgs is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with a firm contracted capacity of 83.1 MMn3/d. It is one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, tgs&#180; infrastructure investments in Vaca Muerta basin will allow to grow significantly in the provision of services to natural gas producers, positioning tgs as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina. tgs shares are traded on NYSE (New York Stock Exchange) and BYMA (Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.). The controlling company of tgs is Compa&#241;&#237;a de Inversiones de Energ&#237;a S.A. (&quot;CIESA&quot;), which owns 51% of the total share capital. CIESA&#8217;s shareholders are: (i) Pampa Energ&#237;a S.A. with 50%, (ii) Grupo Investor Petroqu&#237;mica S.L. (member of the GIP group, led by the Sielecki family) and PCT L.L.C. with the remaining 50%.</I></B></P>
