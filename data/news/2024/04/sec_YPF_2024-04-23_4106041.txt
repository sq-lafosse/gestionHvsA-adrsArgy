<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On April&nbsp;19, 2024, the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York (the &#147;District Court&#148;) issued an order in the
proceedings brought by Petersen Energ&iacute;a Inversora S.A.U., Petersen Energ&iacute;a S.A.U., Eton Park Capital Management, L.P., Eton Park Master Fund, LTD. and Eton Park Fund, L.P. (collectively, &#147;Plaintiffs&#148;) against the Argentine
Republic (the &#147;Republic&#148;) and YPF S.A. (&#147;YPF&#148;), which are currently pending on appeal in the United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On April&nbsp;1, 2024, Plaintiffs filed two motions under seal (i.e., filed with the court confidentially and not available to the public or YPF): one seeking
turnover of the YPF Class&nbsp;D shares held by the Republic; and the other seeking to keep all briefing related to the turnover motion under seal. The Republic filed a response opposing Plaintiffs&#146; request to keep such briefing under seal. YPF
separately filed a letter opposing Plaintiffs&#146; request to keep such briefing under seal. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In its April&nbsp;19 order, the District Court denied
Plaintiffs&#146; request to seal the briefing related to Plaintiffs&#146; turnover motion and ordered Plaintiffs and the Republic to publicly file all documents relating to such motion. On April&nbsp;22, 2024, Plaintiffs filed their turnover motion
publicly, which requests that the District Court order the Republic to turn over the YPF Class&nbsp;D shares held by the Republic to Plaintiffs in partial satisfaction of the District Court&#146;s judgment against the Republic in this proceeding.
The Republic has until May&nbsp;16, 2024 to file its brief in opposition to Plaintiffs&#146; turnover motion. Plaintiffs have until May&nbsp;30, 2024 to file their reply brief. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">YPF is not a party to the turnover motion and was not granted access to the motion or the related documents until they were publicly filed. Following the
filing of the Republic&#146;s opposition brief and the Plaintiffs&#146; reply brief, the District Court may hold oral hearings prior to rendering a decision on the turnover motion. Furthermore, the District Court&#146;s decision on the turnover
motion may be appealed by Plaintiffs or the Republic in accordance with applicable procedural rules. </P>
