rate before the date on which each of the above mentioned instalments is made available according to the payment schedule duly fixed by
the Board; and (ii) to delegate to the Board: (a) the separation of the relevant amount of the Optional Reserve Fund for Future Profit
Distributions to pay the dividend after receiving the approval from the above mentioned entity; and (b) the powers to determine the time,
currency, kind and other terms and conditions of payment pursuant to the scope of the delegation decided by this Shareholders&rsquo; Meeting.</P>
