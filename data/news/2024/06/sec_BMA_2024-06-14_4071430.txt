<P STYLE="text-align: center; font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt"><B>Payment of Cash Dividend</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 28.35pt 0pt 42.55pt; text-align: center">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">We hereby inform to the
Shareholders of Banco Macro S.A. that, pursuant to (i) the resolutions adopted by the General and Special Shareholders&rsquo; Meeting
held on April 12<SUP>th</SUP> 2024, (ii) Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 7984 and Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 7997 authorizing the financial
entities with authorization of the Central Bank of the Republic of Argentina to distribute dividends in 3 equal and consecutive monthly
instalments expressed in constant currency, (iii) the authorization by the Superintendency of Financial and Exchange Entities of the
Central Bank of the Republic of Argentina obtained last May 6<SUP>th</SUP>, and (iv) the resolutions adopted by the Board of Directors
at the meeting held on June 13<SUP>th</SUP> 2024, Banco Macro S.A. shall make available and pay to its shareholders instalment No. 2
of the cash dividend of AR $168,541,001,211.86, (i.e., AR $263.5869049712 per share representing 26,358.6905% of the capital stock of
AR $639,413,408).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Available as of 06/26/2024</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Record date: 06/25/2024</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">As to the aggregate amount of
dividends Banco Macro S.A. shall distribute, please be advised that such amount is subject to a 7% withholding under section 97 of the
Income Tax Law as revised in 2019.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Such cash dividend shall be
made available as of the above stated date at Caja de Valores S.A. located in 25 de Mayo 362, Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Mondays
to Fridays from 10 AM to 3 PM.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Shareholders holding American
Depositary Receipts (ADRs) shall receive the relevant dividend under the applicable depositary agreement through The Bank of New York
Mellon, as depositary agent of such certificates&rsquo; underlying Class B Shares as of the relevant date under the rules applicable in
the jurisdiction in which such ADRs of the Company are listed.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">We further inform to the non-resident
shareholders that they may choose to apply the amount of dividends to the subscription of &ldquo;Bonds for the Reconstruction of a Free
Argentina&rdquo; (BOPREALs), Series 3 issued by the CENTRAL BANK OF THE REPUBLIC OF ARGENITNA (BCRA), pursuant to the provisions of Communique
 &ldquo;A&rdquo; 7997 of the BCRA.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify"></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Each non-resident shareholder
who chooses this option (hereinafter, the &ldquo;Subscribing Shareholder&rdquo;) shall send to the email address <U>productosfinancieros@macro.com.ar</U>,
at least forty-eight (48) business hours in advance of the record date, the following information and/or documents: a) an irrevocable
instruction letter indicating its intention to subscribe BOPREALs with the funds to be received by such shareholders as dividend under
this dividend payment notice. Such instruction letter shall be sent: i) in the case of legal entities, by its legal representative in
the Republic of Argentina with such representative&rsquo;s signature and powers duly certified by notary public and legalized as applicable,
and with proof of such legal entity&rsquo;s registration with the Public Registry of Commerce or signed by the legal representative of
the foreign company or agent pursuant to the power of attorney granted abroad with all required formalities, in compliance with all legal
authentication requirements applicable in the country of origin as well as with the relevant rules and regulations applicable in the
Republic of Argentina to render any document issued in other countries valid in the Republic of Argentina, duly apostilled or certified
by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, as applicable; and ii) in the case of individuals, through a letter signed before a notary public
and legalized as applicable; b) the amount in Argentine pesos for which the Shareholder intends subscribe BOPREALs, which amount shall
not exceed the amount in Argentine pesos such Shareholder is to receive as dividend, after: i) the 7% tax withholding under section 97
of the Income Tax Law, as revised in 2019; and ii) the 17.5% tax withholding as Tax for an Inclusive and Solidarity-based Argentina (PA&Iacute;S)
under Decree No. 385/2024; c) proof of Shareholder&rsquo;s non-resident condition; and d) the custody account to which the Company shall
send the bonds under Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 7997 issued by the BCRA.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Banco Macro undertakes to participate
in the BOPREAL bid immediately following the dividend payment date for the amount requested &ndash;less the above mentioned 7% tax withholding
under section 97 of the Argentine Income Tax Law, as revised in 2019 and the 17.5% withholding as Tax for an Inclusive and Solidarity-based
Argentina (PA&Iacute;S)- for each Subscribing Shareholder and once credited, it shall transfer the relevant BOPREAL to the custody account
informed by the Subscribing Shareholder, under the terms of Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 7997.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">In the event the BCRA does not
call for a BOPREAL bid in which the Subscribing Shareholder is able to participate within 30 calendar days from the date of this notice
Banco Macro S.A. shall return the relevant amount of Argentine pesos to the Subscribing Shareholder to the account the latter may indicate,
free of interests.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 42.45pt 0pt 28.35pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Sending an email to the email
address mentioned above informing the Shareholder&rsquo;s decision to choose this regime, implies Banco Macro S.A. shall no be responsible
in case that &ndash;for any reason whatsoever- the Subscribing Shareholder is unable to get the BOPREALs.</P>
