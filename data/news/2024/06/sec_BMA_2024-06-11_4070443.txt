<P STYLE="text-indent: 0; font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

challenges the fee charged as &ldquo;Monthly Account Statement&rdquo; on all the checking accounts existing in all branches of Banco Macro
S.A. located in the Province of Buenos Aires. Plaintiff seeks to obtain the nullity of such fee and the recovery by all customers of all
amounts charged under such description, plus applicable interests.</P>

<P STYLE="text-indent: 0; font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="text-indent: 0; font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">The complaint

S.A. deems there is low probability of getting an unfavorable court decision in this dispute, and even if that were the case, such unfavorable
