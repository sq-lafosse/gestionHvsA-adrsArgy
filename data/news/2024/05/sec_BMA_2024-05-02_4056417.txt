<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">Please be
advised that on the date hereof BANCO MACRO S.A. has been given notice of a class action complaint filed against it and entitled &ldquo;Protegiendo
al Consumidor P.A.C. vs. Banco Macro S.A. on Recovery of Amounts Paid&rdquo; (Court File #MP-6075/2024).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">Plaintiff
challenges the fee charged as &ldquo;Check Query&rdquo; on all the checking accounts existing in all branches of Banco Macro S.A. located
in the Province of Buenos Aires. Plaintiff seeks to obtain the nullity of such fee and the recovery by all customers of all amounts charged
under such description, plus applicable interests and legal costs.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">The complaint
was brought and is pending before the Court in Civil and Commercial Matters No. 11 of Mar del Plata.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt auto; text-align: justify; background-color: white">Banco Macro
S.A. deems there is low probability of getting an unfavorable court decision in this dispute, and even if that were the case, such unfavorable
decision would not have a material impact on its assets.</P>
