<P STYLE="font: 16pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center; text-indent: 7.1pt; color: #1F497D"><B>PRESS RELEASE
</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>EDENOR increases investment level by 27% compared
to the first quarter of the previous year. </B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>Improvement in the Company&#8217;s distribution margin.</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify"><B>Buenos Aires, May 10, 2024, </B>The Board
of Directors of Empresa Distribuidora y Comercializadora Norte S.A. (&#8220;<B>Edenor</B>&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) approved
today its financial statements for the period ended March 31, 2024, which show a profit of ARS 50,867 million.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">The electricity rate adjustments implemented
since February 16, 2024 imply a significant improvement in <B>Edenor</B>'s distribution margin, balancing the Company's finances, including
going concern. Likewise, allow <B>Edenor</B> to continue making investments to improve service quality, with technology and innovations,
promoting a responsible and efficient use of energy, which was reflected in the levels achieved by our technical service quality global
indicators (SAIDI and SAIFI), as well as the customers&#8217; satisfactory perception of the service received.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0; text-align: center"><IMG SRC="edn202405106k2_004.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0"></P>

<P STYLE="margin: 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 14.2pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Furthermore, the Company has modified the corporate
purpose of its Bylaws, according to its article 4, in line with its adaptation and transformation strategy linked to the challenges of
the energy transition, in order to adapt its electricity distribution business by opening the spectrum to other segments such as the generation
of renewable or conventional electrical energy, as well as critical minerals, among others. This amendment was approved by the Company's
Shareholders' Meeting held on April 25 and ENRE through Resolution 271/2024 of May 9, 2024.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>MAIN FINANCIAL INDICATORS FIRST QUARTER 2024</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">The prior-year period&#8217;s figures have
been restated to reflect the changes in the purchasing power of the Argentine peso, in accordance with International Accounting Standard
No. 29 and the provisions of General Resolution No. 777/2018 of the National Securities Commission.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 28.35pt 0 21.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0; text-align: center"><IMG SRC="edn202405106k2_005.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="font: 6pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 28.35pt 0 7.1pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">Both, <B>revenue</B> and the <B>distribution
margin </B>reflected increases in real terms of 2% and 54%, respectively, compared to the same period of the previous year, as a consequence
of the increase in the CPD (Distribution Own Cost), resulting from the new electricity rate schedules effective as of February 16, 2024
and lower demand due to record high temperatures during the first quarter of 2023, which was offset by the effect of higher energy costs
due to the reduction of subsidies.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">The <B>EBITDA</B> resulted in a profit of
ARS 4,396 million, as a result of the higher income recorded in the last month and a half due to electricity rate restoring, which impacted
an improvement in the distribution margin, which allows the Company to meet its main operating expenses.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">The <B>profit for the period</B> as of March
31, 2024 amounted to ARS 50,867 million, due mainly to both the electricity rate increase, and the consequent restoring of the CPD (Distribution
Own Cost), and the optimization of operating costs.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">As for <B>Investments</B>, in the first quarter
of 2024, they amounted to ARS 49,088 million, which in constant values represents 27% more than the investments recorded in the first
three months of 2023. This indicates Edenor's unwavering commitment to the improvement in the quality of the service, which is seen in
the significant improvements that have been achieved in our operating indicators.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify"><B>OPERATING INDICATORS</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify"><B>Electricity sales</B> in the first three
months of 2024 decreased 7.8% to 5,981 GWh, as compared to the 6,488 GWh sold in the first three months of the previous year, whereas
the quantity of customers increased by 1.0%.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify"><B>Energy losses</B> decreased 8.9% in the
first three months of 2024, as compared to the previous period.</P>
