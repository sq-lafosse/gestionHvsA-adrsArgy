<DIV style=margin-left:85.05pt;width:446.55pt><P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with a firm-contracted capacity of 83.1 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d. We are one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, our infrastructure investment in the Vaca Muerta formation places us as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina.</I></P>
