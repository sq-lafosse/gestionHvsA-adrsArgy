<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0">*The prior-year period&#8217;s figures have been restated to reflect the changes in the

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>Buenos Aires, November 8th, 2024, </B>The Board of Directors
of Empresa Distribuidora y Comercializadora Norte S.A. (&#8220;<B>Edenor</B>&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) approved today its financial
statements for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2024, which show a profit of Ps. 235,069 million.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Taking into consideration the impact of the transitory tariffs
adjustments that have been implemented, the results of operations for the first nine months of 2024 reflect an operating and financial
improvement, which allowed the Company to continue making the investments necessary for maintaining and improving the quality of its services,
through technology and innovations, promoting a responsible and efficient use of energy, meeting the payments for energy purchases since
the electricity tariff have begun to be normalized. This is reflected in the levels of our SAIDI and SAIFI indicators, as well as in the
satisfaction levels of our users.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Furthermore, the Company continues to advance with the Tariff Review
Process in accordance with the task schedule set by the ENRE, which includes the transitory tariff automatic adjustments made from May
through July 2024, that had been postponed, followed by nominal increases in the CPD or VAD (&#8220;Value Added for Distribution&#8221;)
have been implemented since August.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">As a result, during the first nine months of 2024, compared to
the same period of the previous year, there was a partial recomposition of <B>revenues</B> and the <B>distribution margin</B>, which is
reflected by increases of 21% and 53%, respectively. The new electricity tariff schedules are effective since February and April, 2024,
later adjusted as described and affected by a slight decrease in the demand compared to 2023, offset by greater energy costs attributable
to the reduction of subsidies to users.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">The <B>EBITDA</B> resulted in the period under consideration a
profit of Ps. 138,342 million, which allows the Company to meet its main operating expenses.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">The <B>profit for the period</B> as of September 30, 2024 amounted
to Ps. 235,069 million, resulting also from an operating costs&acute; efficiency.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>
