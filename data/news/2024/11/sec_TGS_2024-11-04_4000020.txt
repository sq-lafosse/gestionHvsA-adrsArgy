<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with average firm-contracted capacity of 83.0 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d.
