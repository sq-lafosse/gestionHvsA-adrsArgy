<P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Effects of inflation</B></P>
<P style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>On December 3, 2018, Law No. 27,468 was enacted, sanctioned on November 15, 2018, by the National Argentine Congress. Among other measures, this law abolishes Presidential Decree No. 1,269/02 &#8212;amended by Presidential Decree No. 664/2003 &#8212;&nbsp;through which the controlling entities (among them the CNV) had been instructed not to accept inflation adjusted financial statements. On December 26, 2018, the CNV issued Resolution No. 777/2018, by virtue of which it instructs companies that offer their shares to the public to apply the financial statements restatement method to a stable currency in line with the provisions of IAS 29 &#8220;Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies&#8221;.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>In accordance with such standards, the restatement of financial statements was restarted as from July 1, 2018. In line with the restatement method, non-currency assets and liabilities are restated by an overall price index issued by the Argentine Federation of Professional Councils in Economic Sciences (&#8220;Argentine Federation&#8221;) since their acquisition date or last inflation adjustment (March 1, 2003).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>Likewise, comparative information included in the financial statements has also been restated, but this fact has not modified the decisions based on the financial information corresponding to those fiscal years.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>For further information, see &#8220;Note 4. Significant Accounting Policies &#8211;&nbsp;d) Restatement to current currency &#8211;&nbsp;Comparative Information&#8221; to the consolidated financial statements as of December 31, 2023.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Rounding </B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>Certain figures included in this Report have been rounded for ease of presentation. Percentage figures included in this Report have not, in all cases, been calculated on the basis of such rounded figures but on the basis of such amounts prior to rounding. For this reason, percentage amounts in this report may vary from those obtained by performing the same calculations using the figures in our Financial Statements. Certain numerical figures shown as totals in some tables may not be an arithmetic aggregation of the figures that preceded them due to rounding.</P>

<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Activities of the Company in 9M2024 and 9M2023</B></P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Revenues</B></P>
<P align=center style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Revenues for 9M2024 increased by Ps. 100,282 million compared to 9M2023, mainly due to the increase in revenues from Natural Gas Transportation and Midstream business segments.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Natural Gas Transportation</B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>During 9M2024 revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation business segment accounted for 35% of <B>tgs</B> total revenues (22% for 9M2023). 84% of total revenues in this segment corresponded to firm contracted capacity services (82% for 9M2023).</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation segment during 9M2024 reached Ps. 284,177 million (Ps. 158,631 million in 9M2023). The increase of Ps. 125,547 million was primarily driven by the positive impact of transitional tariff increases received during 2024, as explained below. This effect was partially offset by the negative impact of inflation adjustment.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>On March 26, 2024, the Issuer and ENARGAS entered into a Tariff Transitory Adjustment Agreement (the &#8220;Tariff Agreement&#8221;), which provides for a 675% transitional adjustment in natural gas transportation tariffs effective as of April 3, 2024, pursuant to Resolution No. 112/2024 issued by ENARGAS (&#8220;Resolution 112&#8221;). Resolution 112 provides that, as from May 2024 and until ENARGAS&#8217;s integral tariff review process for the natural gas industry is completed, tariffs are adjusted monthly based on a composite index (the &#8220;Transitional Adjustment Index&#8221;) by the wage index, the wholesale price index and the construction cost index.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>During the following months, ENARGAS notified the licensee companies of the postponement of previously scheduled monthly adjustment. Additionally, it announced that the Transitional Adjustment Index would be replaced by an adjustment index based on expected inflation to be communicated by the Ministry of Economy. The difference between actual and estimated inflation will be considered in the RTI tariff calculation. According to the notification received, if the actual inflation is higher or lower than the inflation estimated by the Ministry of Economy, this difference will be taken into account when determining the tariff resulting from the RTI process.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>On August 1, September 2, October 1 and November 4, 2024 ENARGAS issued new transition tariff charts contemplating increases of 4%, 1%, 2.7% and 3.5%, respectively, over the tariff in effect at the time.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>In addition, Resolution 112 provides that during 2024 <B>tgs</B> must execute an investment plan in the amount of Ps. 27,690 million (adjustable by the Transitional Adjustment Index), which, as of the date hereof, is in the process of execution.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>During 9M2024, revenues corresponding to this business segment represented 48% of total revenues (60% for 9M2023).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>Revenues derived from the Liquids Production and Commercialization business segment amounted to Ps. 367,991 million in 9M2024 (Ps 55,268 million lower than those recorded in 9M2023). This negative effect was mainly due to the decrease in: (i) tons of ethane dispatched, (ii) the price of the ethane and (iii) the volumes of propane, butane and natural gasoline. These effects were partially offset by the positive variation in the real exchange rate, the improvements in the premiums corresponding to the export of propane and butane and in the international reference prices. </P>

<P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-left:108pt;color:#0070C0'><KBD style='position:absolute;text-align:right;margin-left:-108pt;width:108pt;font:10pt Verdana'><B>10.</B></KBD><KBD style=margin-left:62.7pt></KBD><B>Outlook</B>&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-left:36pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>The future of natural gas as an essential fuel for the country's energy matrix is consolidated year after year, which will make Argentina a sustainable country with high growth. We have consolidated ourselves as an integrated service provider in the hydrocarbon industry.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>Our strategy aims to position ourselves in a leading role, carrying out vital undertakings for the future of the country, thus consolidating the growth strategy with a leap of magnitude. With this objective, we are adopting an innovative vision, seeking new business opportunities for our clients with a focus on Vaca Muerta.</P>
<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=center style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0'>8</P>
<HR style='page-break-after:always;border:0;height:3pt;background-color:#909090;margin:8pt 0'><P style=line-height:0;margin:0></P>
<P align=right style='font:10pt Times New Roman;margin:0'><B><IMG src=tgs_6.jpg width=156 height=55 alt='Imagen 6' title='Imagen 6'></B>&nbsp;</P>
<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>Continuing our growth plan in the area, we expect, by the last quarter of 2024, the culmination of the expansion of the Tratay&#233;n conditioning plant through the installation of the remaining conditioning module of 6.6 million m3/day. The total investment made for the installation of this module and the one currently in operation, mentioned above, will amount to US$350 million.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>The current economic situation and the tariff review process faced by the authorities implies that we must continue to generate valid and constructive channels of dialogue that allow us to manage a profitable and efficient natural gas transportation business in accordance with the country's energy development, considering the needs of the internal and external demands and of our stakeholders.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>In this framework, we will be attentive to the talks with the national authorities in order to advance in the concretion of the new RTI process.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>In the Liquids Production and Commercialization Segment, the strategy will be aimed at optimizing the production mix that allows prioritizing those products and distribution channels that provide higher margins, and to maximizing access to the RTP at reasonable costs. For this, it will be very important to be efficient in the management of our assets, ensuring a coordinated, safe and efficient operation.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>In financial terms, and given the aforementioned facts, we will continue to prudently manage our funds in order to preserve our shareholders&#180; value given the volatile macroeconomic context in which our activities will be carried out. </P>

To this end, the Company will carry out actions aimed at the reduction of cost without affecting the reliability and availability of the pipeline system. <P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Business Overview</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Times New Roman;margin:0;text-indent:14.2pt;margin-right:28.3pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (&#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) is one of the companies created as a result of the privatization of Gas del Estado S.E. (&#8220;GdE&#8221;). <B>tgs</B> commenced operations on December 28, 1992 and it is mainly engaged in the Transportation of Natural Gas, and Natural Gas Liquids Production and Commercialization (&#8220;Liquids&#8221;). TGS&#8217;s pipeline system connects major natural gas fields in southern and western Argentina with natural gas distributors and industries in those areas and in the greater Buenos Aires area. The natural gas transportation license to operate this system was exclusively granted to TGS for a period of thirty-five years (&#8220;the License&#8221;). TGS is entitled to a one-time extension of ten years provided that it has essentially met the obligations imposed by the License and by <I>Ente Nacional Regulador del Gas</I> (National Gas Regulatory Body or &#8220;ENARGAS&#8221;). The General Cerri Gas Processing Complex (the &#8220;Cerri Complex&#8221;), where TGS processes natural gas to obtain liquids, was transferred from GdE along with the gas transmission assets. TGS also provides midstream services, which mainly consist of gas treatment, removal of impurities from the natural gas stream, gas compression, wellhead gas gathering and pipeline construction, operation and maintenance services. In addition, telecommunications services are provided through the subsidiary Telcosur S.A. (&#8220;Telcosur&#8221;). These services consist of data transmission services through a network of terrestrial and digital radio relay.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>Subsequently, the corporate purpose of the Company was modified in order to incorporate the development of complementary activities, incidental, linked and / or derived from natural gas transportation, such as the generation and commercialization of electric power and the provision of other services for the hydrocarbon sector in general.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>Although after the new government took office in December 2023, certain restrictions were eased and other changes are expected, the context of volatility and uncertainty continues as of the date of issuance of these interim condensed consolidated financial statements. <P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt;color:#0070C0'><B>17.2 Fair value measurement hierarchy and estimates</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>According to IFRS Accounting Standard 13, the fair value hierarchy introduces three levels of inputs based on the lowest level of input significant to the overall fair value. These levels are:</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 1:</B> </FONT>includes financial assets and liabilities whose fair values are estimated using quoted prices (unadjusted) in active markets for identical assets and liabilities. The instruments included in this level primarily include balances in mutual funds and public or private bonds listed on the <I>Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.</I> (&#8220;BYMA&#8221;). &nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 2:</B> </FONT>includes financial assets and liabilities whose fair value is estimated using different assumptions quoted prices included within Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly (i.e. as prices) or indirectly (for example, derived from prices). &nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 3:</B> </FONT>includes financial instruments for which the assumptions used in estimating fair value are not based on observable market information.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:35.45pt'>- 25.8% adjusted by the Construction Cost Index in Greater Buenos Aires - Materials chapter published by INDEC.</P>
