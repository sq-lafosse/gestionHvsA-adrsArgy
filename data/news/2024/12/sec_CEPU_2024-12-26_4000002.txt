<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0">Dear Mr./Mrs.,</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 5.15pt 0 0; text-indent: 2cm; text-align: justify">I am pleased to write
to you, in my capacity as responsible for market relations of Central Puerto S.A. (the &quot;<U>Company</U>&quot; or &quot;<U>CPSA</U>&quot;),
in compliance with the provisions of Article 2 of Chapter I, Title XII of the Regulations of the National Securities Commission, in order
to inform you that on this date, CPSA has subscribed for 55,000,000 shares equivalent to 27.5% of the capital stock and voting rights
of 3C Lithium Pte. Ltd. (&quot;<U>3C</U>&quot;), (a company incorporated under the laws of Singapore), which owns 100% of the capital
stock of Minera Cordillera S.A. (&quot;<U>MCSA</U>&quot;), an Argentine company that holds the mining rights to the &quot;Tres Cruces&quot;
project. located in the province of Catamarca, Argentina.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 5.15pt 0 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 5.15pt 0 0; text-align: justify">The &quot;Tres Cruces&quot; project is
a recently discovered lithium deposit, with the objective of developing exploration, drilling and exploitation activities of the mineral.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 2cm">The funds provided in this transaction
are intended to finance drilling activities, payments for outstanding mining properties, additional exploration and working capital for
the initial development of the project.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 2cm">This agreement reinforces CPSA's
strategic commitment to the expansion of its activities in exportable goods projects where comparative advantages are identified, and
allows the development of mining in the country, which poses challenges in the energy transition</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 5.15pt 0 5.95pt; text-align: justify; text-indent: 70.9pt">&nbsp;</P>
