Summary .01
 &#x2022; THE BANK&#x2019;S NET INCOME totaled Ps.460 billion in
4Q23. This result was 3894% higher than the Ps.11.5
billion posted in 3Q23 and 789% higher than the Ps.51.7
billion posted in 4Q22. In FY203 net income totaled
Ps.588 billion, 338% higher than in FY2022. In 4Q23, the
accumulated annualized return on average equity
(&#x201C;ROAE&#x201D;) and the accumulated annualized return on
average assets (&#x201C;ROAA&#x201D;) were 33.2% and 8.7%,
respectively.
 &#x2022; In 4Q23, OPERATING INCOME (before G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.1.3 trillion, 142% or
Ps.778 billion higher than in 3Q23 and 224% or Ps.915.5
billion higher than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 4Q23, OPERATING INCOME (after G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.1 trillion, 189% or Ps.679
billion higher than in 3Q23 and 327% or Ps.796 billion
higher than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 4Q23, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL FINANCING
increased 4% or Ps.66 billion quarter over quarter
(&#x201C;QoQ&#x201D;) totaling Ps.1.8 trillion and decreased 2% or
Ps.30.4 billion year over year (&#x201C;YoY&#x201D;). In FY203 both peso
an USD financing decreased 6% and 78% respectively.
 &#x2022; In 4Q23, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL DEPOSITS increased
9% or Ps.290.4 billion QoQ and decreased 16% or
Ps.663.7 billion YoY, totaling Ps.3.37 trillion and
representing 72% of the Bank&#x2019;s total liabilities. Private
sector deposits increased 13% or Ps.360.8 billion QoQ. In
FY2023, both Peso and USD deposits decreased, 33% and
58% respectively.
 &#x2022; Banco Macro continued showing a strong solvency
ratio, with an EXCESS CAPITAL of Ps.1.35 trillion, 35.4%
Capital Adequacy Ratio &#x2013; Basel III and 32.8 % Tier 1 Ratio.
In addition, the Bank&#x2019;s LIQUID ASSETS remained at an
adequate level, reaching 118% of its total deposits in
4Q23.
 &#x2022; In 4Q23, the Bank&#x2019;s NON-PERFORMING TO TOTAL
FINANCING RATIO was 1.29% and the COVERAGE RATIO
reached 200.91%.
 &#x2022; As of 4Q23, through its 519 branches and 9.192
employees Banco Macro serves 4.96 million retail
