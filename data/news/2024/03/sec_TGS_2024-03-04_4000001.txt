<DIV style=margin-left:85.05pt;width:446.55pt><P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with a firm-contracted capacity of 83.4 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d. We are one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, our infrastructure investment in the Vaca Muerta formation places us as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina.</I></P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Our shares are traded on NYSE (New York Stock Exchange) and BYMA (Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.).</I></P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0'><I>Our controlling company is Compa&#241;&#237;a de Inversiones de Energ&#237;a S.A. (&quot;CIESA&quot;), which owns 51% of the total share capital. CIESA&#8217;s shareholders are: (i) Pampa Energ&#237;a S.A. with 50%, (ii) led by the Sielecki family, Grupo Investor Petroqu&#237;mica S.L. (GIP), and PCT L.L.C. hold the remaining 50%.</I></P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>During the 4Q2023, total comprehensive loss amounted to Ps. 23,461 million, or a Ps. 31.17 loss per share (Ps. 155.84 per ADS), compared to a total comprehensive income of Ps. 16,697 million, or Ps. 22.17 per share (Ps. 110.83 per ADS) in the fourth quarter ended on December 31, 2022 (&#8220;4Q2022&#8221;).</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt'>Operating profit for 4Q2023 totaled Ps. 22,647 million, which was Ps. 7,968 million below 4Q2022. This variation was mainly due to lower revenues from the Production and Commercialization of Natural Gas Liquids (Liquids) and Natural Gas Transportation segments amounting to Ps. 26,124 million and Ps. 6,492 million, respectively.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt'>These effects were partially offset by higher revenues from Midstream and Other Services segment (&#8220;Midstream&#8221;) amounting to Ps. 11,735 million and lower operating costs of Ps. 13,238 million.</P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><B>Highlights during 4Q2023 and beyond</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:35.45pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-28.85pt'>&#216;</KBD>In December 2023 the Mercedes - Cardales natural gas pipeline began operations. Through this complementary work to the President Nestor Kirchner natural gas pipeline (<B>&#8220;GPNK&#8221;</B>), carried out by Energ&#237;a Argentina S.A. (&#8220;ENARSA&#8221;), more flexibility is provided to transfer natural gas between the transportation systems operated by <B>tgs</B> and Transportadora de Gas del Norte S.A. in the Great Buenos Aires area. It should be noted that <B>tgs</B> was contracted by ENARSA for the operation and maintenance of this natural gas pipeline.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:35.45pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:35.45pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-28.85pt'>&#216;</KBD>On December 14, 2023, ENARGAS&#8217; Resolution No. 704/2023 called for a public hearing hold on January 8, 2024, in order to submit for consideration a transitory tariff increase. As of the date of this press release, the corresponding regulations authorizing a resulting transitory tariff increase have not been issued.&nbsp;</P>
