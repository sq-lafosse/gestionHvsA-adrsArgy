<P STYLE="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">We are hereby writing to you in order to comply with the requirements of the Argentine Securities Commission and the corresponding rules of ByMA and MAE in

prioritizes assets of scale that provide greater profitability and resilience at different scenarios, the Company&#146;s Board of Directors, at its meeting held on February&nbsp;29, approved the optimization plan (through transfers and reversions)
of certain groups of Conventional Upstream assets - usually called &#147;Mature Fields&#148;. The Mature Fields comprising the asset group consist of a total of 55 areas (the &#147;<B>Assets</B>&#148;). </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The abovementioned plan is consistent with the Company&#146;s new management plans, which considers that the optimization of the conventional Upstream
portfolio is one of the drivers on which the YPF&#146;s strategy is based, with focus on activities and investments in unconventional fields, with the ultimate objective of maximizing value for the Company, its shareholders and investors. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Finally, it is informed that pursuant to the International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) this decision will imply an assessment of the accounting
recoverable value of the Assets, evidencing an impairment indicator of its accounting value as of December&nbsp;31, 2023. Consequently, a <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">non-cash</FONT> impairment is estimated as of the date hereof at approximately
USD 1,800&nbsp;million (or approximately USD 1,200&nbsp;million after taxes), which shall be considered in the annual financial statements of the Company as of December&nbsp;31, 2023, to be informed in due course in accordance with applicable
regulation. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">This estimate is based on preliminary information and subject to change during the ordinary review and audit process of the financial

the information and analysis to date, and is not aware of any factors that could materially affect such estimate. Additionally, this communication includes forward-looking statements. These statements represent the Company&#146;s current
