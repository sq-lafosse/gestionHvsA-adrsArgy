<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt;color:#000000'>I am writing to you in my capacity as Head of Market Relations of Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (&#8220;tgs&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;), in order to report that in its meeting held today, the Board of Directors approved (i) the gas reference prices for SPOT purchases for Plant Thermal Reduction with Related Parties for the 2026 summer and winter periods (the &#8220;Reference Prices&#8221;) and (ii) the extension of the &#8220;Policy for LPG and Natural Gasoline/Raw Gasoline sale and resale operations&#8221; (the &#8220;Policy&#8221;), valid from January 1, 2026 until December 31, 2026.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>Prior to approval of the Reference Prices and the Policy, tgs Audit Committee was asked to issue two reports in compliance with section 72 of Capital Markets Act no. 26,831. </P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>On December 11, 2025, the Audit Committee concluded and informed the Board of Directors that the transactions with Related Parties carried out pursuant to and under the terms of the Reference Prices and the Policy can be deemed to be reasonably entered into on an arm&#8217;s length basis.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>The Audit Committee&#8217;s reports are available for consultation by accredited shareholders if so desired, upon request via email at: <FONT style='color:#0000FF;border-bottom:1px solid #0000FF'>direcciondelegales@tgs.com.ar</FONT>.</P>
