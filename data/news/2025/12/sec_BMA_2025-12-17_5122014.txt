<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center"><B>Payment of Cash Dividend</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; text-align: justify; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">We hereby inform to the Shareholders
of Banco Macro S.A. that, pursuant to (i)&nbsp;the resolutions adopted by the General and Special Shareholders&rsquo; Meeting held on
April&nbsp;4<SUP>th</SUP> 2025, (ii)&nbsp;Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 8214 authorizing the financial entities with authorization of the
Central Bank of the Republic of Argentina to distribute dividends in 10 equal and consecutive monthly instalments expressed in constant
currency, (iii)&nbsp;the authorization by the Superintendency of Financial and Exchange Entities of the Central Bank of the Republic of
Argentina obtained last June&nbsp;4<SUP>th</SUP>, and (iv)&nbsp;the resolutions adopted by the Board of Directors at the meeting held
on December&nbsp;17th 2025, Banco Macro S.A. shall make available and pay to its shareholders instalment No.&nbsp;7 of the cash dividend
of AR $38,372,817,156.73, (i.e., AR $60.0147000927 per share representing 6,001.4700% of the outstanding capital stock of AR $639,390,301).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; text-align: justify; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Available as of: 12/30/2025</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Record date: 12/29/2025</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; text-align: justify; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">As to the aggregate amount of dividends
Banco Macro S.A. shall distribute, please be advised that such amount is subject to a 7% withholding under section 97 of the Income Tax
Law as revised in 2019.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; text-align: justify; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Such cash dividend shall be made available
as of the above stated date at Caja de Valores S.A. located in 25 de Mayo 362, Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Mondays to Fridays from
10 AM to 3 PM.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; text-align: justify; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Shareholders holding American Depositary
Receipts (ADRs) shall receive the relevant dividend under the applicable depositary agreement through The Bank of New York Mellon, as
depositary agent of such certificates&rsquo; underlying Class&nbsp;B Shares as of the relevant date under the rules&nbsp;applicable in
the jurisdiction in which such ADRs of the Company are listed.</P>
