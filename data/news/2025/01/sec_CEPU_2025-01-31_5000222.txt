owns the Diablillos silver-gold project located in northwestern Argentina.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 13pt/107% Times New Roman,serif; margin: 7.85pt 5.45pt 0 5.05pt; text-align: justify">The completion of the transaction

about February 6, 2025. CPSA's current shareholding, through Proener S.A.U., in AbraSilver is 4%, which, upon completion of the transaction,
will amount to 9.9%.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 13pt/107% Times New Roman,serif; margin: 7.95pt 5.45pt 0 5.05pt; text-align: justify">AbraSilver owns 100% of the shares
of the Diablillos silver and gold project located in the provinces of Salta and Catamarca. The current proven resources and</P>
