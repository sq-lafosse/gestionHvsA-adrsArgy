Date, but which may change without notice (the &#147;<U>Settlement Date</U>&#148;). Payment for the Securities that are validly tendered and accepted for purchase pursuant to the Tender Offer will be made either on the Settlement Date or on the date
referred to as the &#147;<U>Guaranteed Delivery Settlement Date,</U>&#148; as applicable. The Guaranteed Delivery Settlement Date in respect of Securities with respect to which a properly completed and duly executed Notice of Guaranteed Delivery is
delivered at or prior to the Expiration Date (to the extent that such Securities are not delivered prior to the Expiration Date) that are accepted by YPF for purchase in the Tender Offer is expected to be on or around Tuesday, January&nbsp;21, 2025,
the third business day after the Expiration Date. YPF will not be responsible for any delays in the transmission of funds to Holders attributable to the clearing systems and under no circumstances will any interest be payable because of any such
