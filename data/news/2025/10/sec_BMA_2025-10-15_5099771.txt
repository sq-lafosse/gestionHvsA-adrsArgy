<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center"><B>Payment
of Cash Dividend</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: center">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">We hereby inform to the Shareholders
of Banco Macro S.A. that, pursuant to (i) the resolutions adopted by the General and Special Shareholders&rsquo; Meeting held on April
4<SUP>th</SUP> 2025, (ii) Communique &ldquo;A&rdquo; 8214 authorizing the financial entities with authorization of the Central Bank of
the Republic of Argentina to distribute dividends in 10 equal and consecutive monthly instalments expressed in constant currency, (iii)
the authorization by the Superintendency of Financial and Exchange Entities of the Central Bank of the Republic of Argentina obtained
last June 4<SUP>th</SUP>, and (iv) the resolutions adopted by the Board of Directors at the meeting held on October 15<SUP>th</SUP> 2025,
Banco Macro S.A. shall make available and pay to its shareholders instalment No. 5 of the cash dividend of AR $ 36,589,874,132.56 , (i.e.,
AR $ 57.2243060958 per share representing 5,722.4306% of the outstanding capital stock of AR $639,411,408).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Available as of: 10/31/2025</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Record date: 10/29/2025</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">We also inform that since the
Bank has a share buyback program in force, in case of any share buyback carried out between the date of this notice and COB (Close of
Business) on the record date, a Supplementary Notice will be filed.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">As to the aggregate amount of
dividends Banco Macro S.A. shall distribute, please be advised that such amount is subject to a 7% withholding under section 97 of the
Income Tax Law as revised in 2019.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Such cash dividend shall be
made available as of the above stated date at Caja de Valores S.A. located in 25 de Mayo 362, Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Mondays
to Fridays from 10 AM to 3 PM.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt; text-align: justify">Shareholders holding American
Depositary Receipts (ADRs) shall receive the relevant dividend under the applicable depositary agreement through The Bank of New York
Mellon, as depositary agent of such certificates&rsquo; underlying Class B Shares as of the relevant date under the rules applicable in
the jurisdiction in which such ADRs of the Company are listed.</P>
