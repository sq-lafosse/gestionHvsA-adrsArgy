<Center><DIV STYLE="width:8.5in" align="left">
 <P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>YPF and Eni take a new step in the development </B></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman" ALIGN="center"><B>of the Argentina LNG Project </B></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">YPF and Eni
signed a new agreement toward the completion of one of the phases of the Argentina LNG project. It was signed in Rome by YPF President and CEO Horacio Mar&iacute;n and Eni CEO Claudio Descalzi. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">&#147;We are making very rapid progress with Eni. We are confident that before the end of the year we will be able to reach the final investment decision and
realize this phase of this project, which is key to our country&#146;s energy future,&#148; emphasized Horacio Mar&iacute;n. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The agreement defines the
required steps to reach the final investment decision for the phase of the project that includes the production, treatment, transportation and liquefaction installations of gas through floating units, for a total capacity of 12&nbsp;million tons of
LNG per year. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">With this new development, YPF continues to take firm steps toward the completion of the Argentina LNG project, which seeks to monetize
Vaca Muerta&#146;s gas resources and transform the country into a reliable global energy exporter. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><U>About Argentina LNG: </U></P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Argentina LNG is a large-scale integrated, upstream and midstream gas development project designed to develop the resources of the onshore &#147;Vaca
Muerta&#148; field and serve international markets. It is expected to reach exports in a phased approach of up to 30&nbsp;million tons per year of LNG by 2030. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><U>About Eni: </U></P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Eni is a global energy company headquartered
in Italy. It operates in more than 60 countries and, through its subsidiaries, is present throughout the energy value chain. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><U><FONT
