<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><B>INCORPORATION BY REFERENCE</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">This Form 6-K and the related exhibits are hereby incorporated by reference
into the registration statement of Grupo Financiero Galicia S.A. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;) on <A HREF="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1114700/000111470025000002/resalef-3.htm">Form F-3</A> (Registration Number 333-284113)
and the related prospectuses, as such Registration Statement and prospectuses may be amended from time to time.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><U>Information Contained in this Report on Form 6-K</U></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">On June 10, 2025, the Company announced the commencement and pricing
of the underwritten secondary offering (the &ldquo;Offering&rdquo;) by HSBC Bank plc (the &ldquo;Selling Shareholder&rdquo;) of 11,721,449
American Depositary Shares (&ldquo;ADSs&rdquo;) representing 117,214,490 Class B ordinary shares of the Company, par value Ps.1.00 per
share (&ldquo;Class B ordinary shares&rdquo;) at a public offering price of $54.25 per ADS. The Company did not sell any ADSs and did
not receive any proceeds from the sale of the ADSs in the Offering. A copy of the press release issued in connection with the launch and
pricing of the Offering is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and Exhibit 99.2, respectively.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The ADSs were sold pursuant to an Underwriting Agreement (the &ldquo;Underwriting
Agreement&rdquo;), dated June 10, 2025, among HSBC Bank plc, as the Selling Shareholder, the Company, Morgan Stanley &amp; Co. LLC and
Goldman Sachs &amp; Co. LLC, as representatives of the several underwriters named therein.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The Underwriting Agreement includes customary representations, warranties
and covenants by the Company and Selling Shareholder, customary conditions to closing, obligations of the parties and termination provisions.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The foregoing description of the Underwriting Agreement does not purport
to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Underwriting Agreement, a copy of which is filed
herewith as Exhibit 1.1 to this Report on Form 6-K (&ldquo;Form 6-K&rdquo;) and is incorporated by reference herein.</P>
