<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 0.4pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">Dear Mr./Mrs.,</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0.8pt; text-align: left; text-indent: 0cm">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">It is a pleasure
to contact you in my capacity as Head of Market Relations of Central&nbsp;Puerto&nbsp;S.A. (&#8220;<U>CPSA</U>&#8221;, &#8220;<U>CEPU</U>&#8221;
or the &#8220;<U>Company</U>&#8221;) in compliance of Section 2, Chapter&nbsp;I, Title XII of CNV Regulations (restated text 2013 as amended)
so as to inform that the Board of Directors decided the following at its Meeting today:</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">(i) To approve
-subject to the approval of the corresponding Shareholders&#8217; Meetings of the involved companies- the corporate reorganization of
the Company and CP Renovables S.A. (&#8220;<U>CPR</U>&#8221;), which shall consist of a merger by absorption where CPR will transfer -in
its capacity as absorbed company- and CPSA will acquire -in its capacity as absorbing and continuing company- ownership over CPR&#8217;s
property (the &#8220;<U>Merger</U>&#8221;) at the Merger&#8217;s effective date. All this under the terms and conditions described in
the Previous Merger Commitment (&#8220;<U>Previous Merger Commitment</U>&#8221;) and the Merger Prospectus (the &#8220;<U>Merger Prospectus</U>&#8221;).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0.4pt 0.5pt; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">Considering
CPSA holds 100% of CPR&#8217;s shares, CPSA&#8217;s equity will not be increased due to the Merger. For that reason, it is not necessary
for CPSA to increase its equity nor to issue new shares due to the Merger. Consequently, it is not necessary to set a shares swap relationship
since all CPR&#8217;s shares will be canceled without the need of new CPSA shares being issued. CPR will be wound up without being liquidated
as a consequence of the Merger.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">The
Merger will be conducted in accordance with the provisions of Section 82 and related ones of the Business Entities Act (Law No.
19550) as amended, it being fiscally framed as a corporate reorganization free of taxes as per Section 80 and related ones of the
Income Tax Act (Law No. 20628) as amended and its Decree (Decree 862/19 as amended), and subject, among other authorizations, to the
administrative agreement of CNV in accordance with Section I, Chapter X, Title II of CNV Regulations (restated text 2013 as
amended).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">In addition,
it is informed that the Board of Directors also decided to approve the following at its Meeting: (i) The use of the financial statements
for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 issued on March 7, 2025 as individual balance sheets for the purpose of the Merger in accordance
with Section 82 and related ones of the Business Entities Act; and (ii) a special merger consolidated balance sheet of CPR and CPSA as
of December 31, 2024; (iii) the Previous Merger Commitment; and (iv) the Merger Prospectus.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">(ii) To approve
-subject to the approval of the corresponding Shareholders&#8217; Meetings of the involved companies- the corporate reorganization of
the Company and Ecogas Inversiones S.A. (&#8220;<U>ECOGAS</U>&#8221;), which shall consist of a split-off-merger, in which CEPU will divide:
(a) The shareholding it has in Distribuidora de Gas del Centro S.A., Energ&iacute;a Sudamericana S.A. -both companies controlled by ECOGAS-,
and in ECOGAS; and (b) the amount of $305,000,000 (jointly, the &#8220;<U>Divided CEPU Equity</U>&#8221;) to merge such equity with ECOGAS,
which company will have the capacity as absorbing and continuing company under the terms and conditions described in the Previous Split-off-Merger
Commitment (the &#8220;<U>Previous Commitment</U>&#8221;) and the Split-off-Merger Prospectus (the &#8220;<U>Prospectus</U>&#8221;). ECOGAS
will see the following: (a) The issuance of 8,097,326 common book-entry shares Class &#8220;D&#8221; under the public offering regime
with a face-value of $10 and holding one vote each, which will be 80,973,264 common book-entry shares Class &#8220;D&#8221; under the
public offering regime with a face-value of $1 and holding one vote each in case ECOGAS Shareholders&#8217; Meeting approves the face
value of the shares (the &#8220;<U>New Shares</U>&#8221;), which shall be granted to CEPU shareholders depending on the swap relationship
agreed by the Parties; (b) the cancellation of the total common book-entry shares class &#8220;A&#8221; of ECOGAS held by CEPU (5,998,658
shares with a face value of $10 each, which will be 59,986,580 shares with a face value of $1 in case ECOGAS Shareholders&#8217; Meeting
approves the face value of the shares; (c) the increase of its share capital by $20,986,684, increasing the amount of $229,230,580 to
$250,217,264 due to the issuance of the New Shares and the cancellation of the shares Class &#8220;A&#8221; referred to in points (a)
and (b) above; and (d) the increase of its equity by the fair value of the Divided CEPU Equity, net of the fair value of shares Class
&#8220;A&#8221;, which will be canceled, and net of fair value of its own shares that may be kept by ECOGAS in the share fractions not
given to CEPU shareholders. The difference between the fair value om point (d) above and the increase of the mentioned share capital shall
be recorded as &#8220;Issuance premium&#8221; (the &#8220;<U>Corporate Reorganization</U>&#8221; or &#8220;<U>Split-off-Merger</U>&#8221;,
indistinctly).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">The Split-off-Merger
will be conducted in accordance with the provisions of Section 83, 88 and related ones of the Business Entities Act (Law No. 19550) as
amended, it being fiscally framed as a corporate reorganization free of taxes as per Section 80 and related ones of the Income Tax Act
(Law No. 20628) as amended and its Decree (Decree&nbsp;862/19 as amended) and subject, among other authorizations, to the administrative
agreement of CNV in accordance with Section I, Chapter X, Title II of CNV Regulations (restated text 2013 as amended).</P>




<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">In addition,
it is informed that the Board of Directors also decided to approve the following at its Meeting: (i) The use of the financial statements
for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 issued on March 7, 2025 as individual balance sheets for the purpose of the Split-off-Merger
in accordance with Sections 83 and 88 of the Business Entities Act; (ii) the special split-off balance sheet of the Company as of December
31, 2024; (iii) the special consolidated split-off-merger balance sheet of ECOGAS and CEPU as of December 31, 2024; (iv) the Previous
Commitment; and (v) the Prospectus.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">Finally,
the Board of Directors at its Meeting decided to call a Special Shareholders&#8217; Meeting to consider the Merger, the Corporate Reorganization
and the amendment of the corporate purpose so that the company can ensure different obligations on May 22, 2025 on first call.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 2.35pt 8.1pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: -0.5pt">Based on
the foregoing, it is put on record that once the Prospectus and the Merger Prospectus project are approved, the Company will publish so
in accordance with Article 5, Section I, Chapter X, Title II of CNV Regulations, together with the corresponding documents that must be
made duly available to the shareholders before the Meeting.</P>
