<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 12pt 0 3pt; text-align: center"><B><I>CENTRAL PUERTO S.A. SUPERVISORY
COMMITTEE</I></B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B><I>2025 ACTION PLAN</I></B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>As per Section 110 of Law No. 26831 and CNV
Regulations, the Supervisory Committee must annually present before the Board of Directors and the Statutory Audit Committee its action
plan for the commencing fiscal year. </I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>The following are the topics to be considered
under the Action Plan for the fiscal year 2025:</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 0"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><B><I>1.</I></B></TD><TD><B><I>Internal Audit</I></B></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>The Committee will review the Internal Audit&rsquo;s
performance by issuing an opinion with the annual financial statements&rsquo; presentation. To such end, the following will be done:</I></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.45pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>To review the Internal Audit&rsquo;s Activities Plan based on risks and their reach and budget, being
the Committee able to suggest changes or expansions if considered proper.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.45pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>To hold meetings periodically with Internal Audit to check compliance with the Plan and to consider
new audit risks to acknowledge possible difficulties for its execution. </I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.45pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>At the meetings hold with Internal Audit, the reports issued in relation to the reviews made by the
area will be considered. In addition, the follow-up of observations stemming from previous reports will be reviewed, as well as the application
of the proposed suggestions by Internal Audit.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0 35.45pt; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 0"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><B><I>2.</I></B></TD><TD><B><I>External Audit</I></B></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee will issue an opinion on the external auditors&rsquo; appointment proposal
(for their continuity or change) to be presented by the Board of Directors at the Annual Shareholders&rsquo; Meeting. In addition, it
will ask for additional and supplementary documentation if necessary, so as to determine the appointment reasonability, checking if the
proposed external auditors&rsquo; independence criteria are met as per the regulations in force.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee will acknowledge the external audit work plan, reviewing the scheduled activity
and proposing, if necessary, changes or adjustment based on the Supervisory Committee&rsquo;s review.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>Meetings will be scheduled to treat accounting information to organize the follow-up of the external
audit&rsquo;s plan development, at least when issuing the Company&rsquo;s quarterly financial statements. Together with auditors, the
difficulties or controversies eventually presented with the Company&rsquo;s Management will be reviewed. </I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>When presenting the annual financial statements, the external auditor&rsquo;s fees will be informed.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 18pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee will review the External Audit&rsquo;s performance by issuing an opinion
with the annual financial statements&rsquo; presentation.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>



<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.85pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 17.85pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee considers whether the External Audit&rsquo;s services comply with SEC and
CNV regulations. The Supervisory Committee will annually perform a review of the commitment, efficiency, and independence of the external
auditor&rsquo;s work. This review will be made based on the information provided by the Company&rsquo;s Management, which will interact
with external auditors and that have the necessary knowledge to define compliance with the exposed parameters and that will later be presented
through a report to the Supervisory Committee to then be considered by the Board of Directors.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0 36pt; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 12pt; margin-bottom: 3pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 0"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 21.3pt"><B><I>3.</I></B></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><B><I>Financial information, material news and functioning of the administrative-accounting system of
internal control and anti-fraud program</I></B></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 14.2pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 21.25pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Committee will review the functioning and continuous improvement of the internal control and administrative-accounting
systems, their reliability and of the financial information provided to the markets and supervision entities and the Company&rsquo;s exposure
to corporate fraud. To such end, periodic meetings will be held with management, Internal and External Audit. At such meetings, the Committee
will review the reports and other documents the Internal and External Audit and Management submit before it. </I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B><I>4. Operations among related parties or
conflict of interest</I></B></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.45pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee, at the Board of Directors request or any of its members, will issue a previous
opinion regarding the operations with related parties involving a relevant amount, taking into account the regulations in force for the
definition of these concepts.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 17.45pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Symbol">&#183;</FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><I>The opinion shall be on whether the operation&rsquo; conditions may be considered reasonable and usual
in the market&rsquo;s practice.</I></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B><I>5. Integrity Plan</I></B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee will: (i) periodically
ask the Compliance Officer a report on the implementation of the Integrity Plan, (ii) approve the plans, codes or procedures added as
exhibits to the Integrity Plan, and (iii) at least every six months, inform the Board of Directors on the news related to the implementation
and updating of the Integrity Plan.</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>If necessary, the Committee will resort to
independent external auditors to support its opinion on this type of operations. The Committee will issue an informed opinion on that
regard.</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>&nbsp;</I></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 10pt"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 3.3pt"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 18pt"><FONT STYLE="line-height: 150%"><B><I>6.</I></B></FONT></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><B><I>Corporate Governance</I></B></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 10pt; text-align: justify"><I>The Supervisory Committee will treat
and, eventually, approve, implement, monitor, and keep updated all the policies related to the Company&rsquo;s corporate governance best
practices, keeping the Board of Directors informed in that regard based on the local and international regulations in force, as well
as the best corporate practices recorded in the sector. Among other polices and as an example, the Company&rsquo;s Insider Trading Policy
and the progress in the implementation of the Company&rsquo;s Integrity Plan (the &ldquo;Program&rdquo;) is mentioned. Moreover, it will
monitor the policies and procedures on cybersecurity.</I></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B><I>&nbsp;</I></B></P>

<TABLE CELLPADDING="0" CELLSPACING="0" WIDTH="100%" STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 0"><TR STYLE="vertical-align: top">
<TD STYLE="width: 0"></TD><TD STYLE="width: 54pt"><B><I>7.</I></B></TD><TD STYLE="text-align: justify"><B><I>Committee&rsquo;s Annual Management Report</I></B></TD></TR></TABLE>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/150% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><I>After the fiscal year closing and when presenting
the annual financial statements, the Committee must issue an annual report on its performance, rendering account of it before the Board
of Directors. </I></P>
