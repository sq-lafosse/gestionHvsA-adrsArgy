<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify">Dear Sirs,</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 1in">Please be advised
that on the date hereof, BANCO MACRO S.A. has been given notice of a class action complaint filed against it and entitled &quot;Protegiendo
al Consumidor P.A.C. vs. Banco Macro S.A. on Recovery of Amounts Paid&quot; (Court File #27668).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 127.6pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 1in">Plaintiff argues
that when calculating the default interests applicable to users of credit cards issued by the Bank, the Bank applies the provisions under
Section 20 of Decree No. 70/2023, which is unconstitutional. In this sense, Plaintiff requests the reimbursement of the amounts collected
by the Bank to all users and consumers in the Province of Buenos Aires as default interests pursuant to the provisions of Section 20 of
the Decree No. 70/2023, in excess of the assessment method provided under the original wording of Section 18 of Law 25065, plus applicable
interests and legal costs.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">The complaint
was brought and is pending before the Court in Civil and Commercial Matters No. 14 of Mar del Plata.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 49.65pt; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">Banco Macro
S.A. deems there is low probability of getting an unfavorable court decision in this dispute, and even if that were the case, such unfavorable
decision would not have a material impact on its assets.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 35.05pt 0pt 49.65pt; text-align: justify; background-color: white">&nbsp;</P>
