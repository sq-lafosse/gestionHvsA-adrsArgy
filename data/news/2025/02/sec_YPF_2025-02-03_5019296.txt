<FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">&#147;Non-current</FONT> Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations.&#148; Furthermore, in accordance with Article 23, Section (a), Paragraph 9 of the Listing Rules of Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A., the

<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Likewise, and as an integral part of the aforementioned transaction, YPF has granted to the Buyers a license and authorization to produce and commercialize
