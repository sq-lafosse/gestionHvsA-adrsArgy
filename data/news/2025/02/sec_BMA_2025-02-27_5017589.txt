Summary .01
 &#x2022; THE BANK&#x2019;S NET INCOME totaled Ps.102.2 billion in
4Q24. This result was 4% or Ps.3.5 billion higherthan the
result posted in 3Q24. In 4Q24, the accumulated
annualized return on average equity (&#x201C;ROAE&#x201D;) and the
accumulated annualized return on average assets
(&#x201C;ROAA&#x201D;) were 7.5% and 2.4%, respectively.
 &#x2022; In 4Q24, OPERATING INCOME (before G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.813.9 billion, 9% or
Ps.81.9 billion lower than in 3Q24 and 72% or Ps.2.1
trillion lower than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 4Q24, OPERATING INCOME (after G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.359.9 billion, 17% or
Ps.76.2 billion lower than in 3Q24 and 84% or Ps.1.9
trillion lower than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 4Q24, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL FINANCING
increased 18% or Ps.884.1 billion quarter over quarter
(&#x201C;QoQ&#x201D;) totaling Ps.5.8 trillion and increased 45% or
Ps.1.8 trillion year over year (&#x201C;YoY&#x201D;). In FY204 both peso
an USD financing increased 14% and 29% respectively.
 &#x2022; In 4Q24, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL DEPOSITS decreased
3% or Ps.299.3 billion QoQ and increased 15% or Ps.1.1
trillion YoY, totaling Ps.8.4 trillion and representing 81%
of the Bank&#x2019;s total liabilities. Private sector deposits
increased 2% or Ps.147 billion QoQ. In FY2024, Peso
deposits increased 2% while USD deposits decreased,
18%.
 &#x2022; Banco Macro continued showing a strong solvency
ratio, with an EXCESS CAPITAL of Ps.2.8 trillion, 32.4%
Capital Adequacy Ratio &#x2013; Basel III and 31.6 % Tier 1 Ratio.
In addition, the Bank&#x2019;s LIQUID ASSETS remained at an
adequate level, reaching 79% of its total deposits in
4Q24.
 &#x2022; In 4Q24, the Bank&#x2019;s NON-PERFORMING TO TOTAL
FINANCING RATIO was 1.28% and the COVERAGE RATIO
reached 158.8%.
 &#x2022; As of 4Q24, through its 515 branches and 9.004
employees Banco Macro serves 6.12 million retail
customers (2.5 million digital customers) across 23 of the
24 Provinces in Argentina and over 201,233 corporate
customers.</font></p></td></tr></table></div><div style="margin-left:2.6515151515151%;margin-right:2.65151515151515%;page-break-after:always;" ><div style="background-color:#000000;clear:both;height:2pt;border:0;margin:30pt 0pt 30pt 0pt;"></div></div><div align="center"><table border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0" style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;"><tr style="page-break-inside:avoid;"><td width="92.3%" valign="top" style="padding:0in 5.4pt 0in 5.4pt;width:92.3%;"><p align="center" style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;text-align:center;"><img src="tm257718d1_6kimg007.jpg" alt="GRAPHIC"></p></td><td width="7.7%" valign="middle" style="padding:0in 5.4pt 0in 5.4pt;width:7.7%;"><p style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;"><font size="1" color="white" face="Arial" style="color:white;font-size:1.0pt;">BANCO MACRO 4Q24 EARNINGS RELEASE
