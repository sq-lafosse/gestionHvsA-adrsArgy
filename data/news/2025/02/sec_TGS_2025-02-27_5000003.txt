<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with average firm-contracted capacity of 83.5 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d. We are one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, our infrastructure investment in the Vaca Muerta formation places us as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina.</I></P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt'><I>Our shares are traded on NYSE (New York Stock Exchange) and BYMA (Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.).</I></P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><B>Highlights during 4Q2024 and beyond</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:7pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'>&#183;</KBD><FONT style=font-size:9pt>ENARGAS published new transitional tariff charts which included increases effective on  December 4, January 1 and February 1, of 3%, 2.5%, and 1.5%, respectively.</FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><FONT style=font-size:9pt> </FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'>&#183;</KBD>On February 6, 2025, a public hearing was held to consider, among other issues, the Five-Year Tariff Review (&quot;RQT&quot;) for natural gas transportation and distribution, as well as the methodology for periodic adjustments for natural gas transportation and distribution tariffs. As of today, ENARGAS resolution with the new tariff charts and the regulatory framework of the RQT, which will be in force for the period 2025 &#8211;&nbsp;2029, is pending issuance.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'>&#183;</KBD>On January 24, 2025, the Secretary of Energy issued Resolution No. 15/2025, which, effective from that date, eliminates the maximum sale price set for products supplied under the <I>Plan Hogar</I> (with the export parity price published by the SE under Law No. 26,020 being the sale price limit). Furthermore, although this resolution maintains the obligation of LPG producers to supply the domestic market, it eliminates the previously existing supply contributions.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'>&#183;</KBD>During February 2025, the construction and commissioning of the second natural gas conditioning module at the Tratay&#233;n Plant was completed, increasing conditioning capacity by 6.6 MMm&#179;/d. With the completion of this last stage of the project which initiated in 2022, the total conditioning capacity of the Tratay&#233;n plant totals 28 MMm&#179;/d.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'>&#183;</KBD>On February 5, 2025, as a result of the upward revision of Argentina's Transfer and Convertibility rating, S&amp;P Global Ratings upgraded tgs's local and international debt credit rating from &quot;CCC&quot; to &quot;B-&quot;.&nbsp;</P>
