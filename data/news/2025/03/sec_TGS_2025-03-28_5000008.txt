<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt;text-indent:0.25pt'>Dear Sirs,</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>I am writing to Comisi&#243;n Nacional de Valores (<I>Argentine Securities and Exchange Commission</I>) and Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A. (<I>Argentine Stock Exchanges and Markets</I>) in my capacity as Head of Market Relations of <B>Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A.</B> (the &#8220;Company&#8221; or &#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221;), to supplement the Material Fact submitted on March 10, 2025, in connection with the floods that hit the City of Bah&#237;a Blanca and surrounding areas, with direct impact on Cerri Complex.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>The main operational objective has been to restore in a reliable and safe manner operations related to the Natural Gas Transportation Public Service. In this respect, I hereby inform that on March 14, after performing the corresponding operating tests, the Fiat Compression Plant was in operating reserve and on March 24 Solar Compression Plant was put into operation.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>Additionally, communications and links between Cerri Complex and the rest of the Company were reestablished. Also, a large part of Cerri Complex got power and high voltage entry and medium and low voltage central distribution were enabled. </P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>Works to power all the facilities and systems are ongoing, as, to date, they are powered by portable equipment due to lack of reliability of the external energy supplier, which was also severely hit by the climatic event.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>Likewise, works to soon put into operation liquids production are ongoing.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin:0'>We will continue to report insofar as we have further details on the matter.</P>
