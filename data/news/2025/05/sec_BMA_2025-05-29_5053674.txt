Summary .01
 &#x2022; THE BANK&#x2019;S NET INCOME totaled Ps.45.7 billion in
1Q25. This result was 59% or Ps.65.3 billion lower than
the result posted in 4Q24. In 1Q25, the annualized return
on average equity (&#x201C;ROAE&#x201D;) and the annualized return on
average assets (&#x201C;ROAA&#x201D;) were 3.8% and 1.2%,
respectively.
 &#x2022; In 1Q25, OPERATING INCOME (before G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.801 billion, 9% or Ps.82.6
billion lower than in 4Q24 and 68% or Ps.1.7 trillion lower
than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 1Q24, OPERATING INCOME (after G&amp;A and
personnel expenses) totaled Ps.347.8 billion, 11% or
Ps.43 billion lower than in 4Q24 and 82% or Ps.1.6 trillion
lower than the same period of last year.
 &#x2022; In 1Q25, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL FINANCING
increased 22% or Ps.1.4 trillion quarter over quarter
(&#x201C;QoQ&#x201D;) totaling Ps.7.7 trillion and increased 97% or
Ps.3.8 trillion year over year (&#x201C;YoY&#x201D;). In 1Q25 both peso
an USD financing increased 21% and 22% respectively.
 &#x2022; In 1Q25, BANCO MACRO&#x2019;S TOTAL DEPOSITS increased
5% or Ps.485.4 billion QoQ and increased 23% or Ps.1.8
trillion YoY, totaling Ps.9.6 trillion and representing 82%
of the Bank&#x2019;s total liabilities. Private sector deposits
increased 4% or Ps.349.6 billion QoQ. In1Q25, Peso
deposits increased 15% while USD deposits decreased,
17%.
 &#x2022; Banco Macro continued showing a strong solvency
ratio, with an EXCESS CAPITAL of Ps.3.2 trillion, 34.3%
Capital Adequacy Ratio &#x2013; Basel III and 33.6% Tier 1 Ratio.
In addition, the Bank&#x2019;s LIQUID ASSETS remained at an
adequate level, reaching 68% of its total deposits in
1Q25.
 &#x2022; In 1Q25, the Bank&#x2019;s NON-PERFORMING TO TOTAL
FINANCING RATIO was 1.44% and the COVERAGE RATIO
reached 163.34%.
 &#x2022; As of 1Q25, through its 505 branches and 8,903
employees Banco Macro serves 6.23 million retail
customers (2.6 million digital customers) across 23 of the
24 Provinces in Argentina and over 205,816 corporate
customers.</font></p></td></tr></table></div><div style="margin-left:2.6515151515151%;margin-right:2.65151515151515%;page-break-after:always;" ><div style="background-color:#000000;clear:both;height:2pt;border:0;margin:30pt 0pt 30pt 0pt;"></div></div><div align="center"><table border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="0" style="width:100%;border-collapse:collapse;"><tr style="page-break-inside:avoid;"><td width="92.3%" valign="top" style="padding:0in 5.4pt 0in 5.4pt;width:92.3%;"><p align="center" style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;text-align:center;"><img src="tm2516377d1_6kimg007.jpg" alt="GRAPHIC"></p></td><td width="7.7%" valign="middle" style="padding:0in 5.4pt 0in 5.4pt;width:7.7%;"><p style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;"><font size="1" color="white" face="Arial" style="color:white;font-size:1.0pt;">BANCO MACRO 1Q25 EARNINGS RELEASE

MEP/CCL are also eliminated

dispatch from the port of origin.

port of origin, and 20% upon customs entry registration.
