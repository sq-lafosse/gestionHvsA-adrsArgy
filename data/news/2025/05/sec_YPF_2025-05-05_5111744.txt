today, YPF S.A., through its subsidiary Sur Inversiones Energ&eacute;ticas S.A.U. (a single-shareholder corporation wholly owned by YPF S.A.) (&#147;<B>SUR</B>&#148;), together with Pan American Energy S.L. (&#147;<B>PAE</B>&#148;), Wintershall DEA
Argentina S.A. (&#147;<B>Wintershall</B>&#148;), Pampa Energ&iacute;a S.A. (&#147;<B>PAMPA</B>&#148;), and Golar FLNG <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">Sub-Holding</FONT> Company Limited (&#147;<B>Golar Subholding</B>&#148;)&#151;all of whom
collectively comprise the shareholders of Southern Energy S.A. (&#147;<B>SESA</B>&#148;)&#151;have agreed to: </P> <P STYLE="font-size:6pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"> <P ALIGN="left" STYLE=" margin-top:0pt ; margin-bottom:0pt; font-family:Times New Roman; font-size:10pt">make the Final Investment Decision (&#147;<B>FID</B>&#148;), as contemplated under the Bareboat Charter Agreement
entered into GOLAR HILLI CORPORATION on July 4, 2024 (as subsequently amended), for the <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">20-year</FONT> charter of the HILLI EPISEYO liquefaction vessel (&#147;<B>FLNG HILLI</B>&#148;), which has a nominal capacity of
2.45&nbsp;million metric tons of LNG per annum (&#147;<B>MTPA</B>&#148;). This vessel will be located off the coast of the Argentine Sea, in the Province of R&iacute;o Negro, to process natural gas from the Vaca Muerta formation for the purpose of
exporting LNG (&#147;<B>BBCA HILLI</B>&#148;). </P></TD></TR></TABLE> <P STYLE="font-size:6pt;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<TD ALIGN="left" VALIGN="top"> <P ALIGN="left" STYLE=" margin-top:0pt ; margin-bottom:0pt; font-family:Times New Roman; font-size:10pt">enter into a second Bareboat Charter Agreement with GOLAR MK II CORPORATION for the construction, charter, and
operation of a new liquefaction vessel, FUJI LNG (&#147;<B>FLNG MKII</B>&#148;), under a <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">20-year</FONT> term (extendable by an additional five-year period at SESA&#146;s option). The FLNG MKII will have a nominal
capacity of 3.5 MTPA, intended to expand the capacity to process gas from Vaca Muerta and export LNG, subject to a future Final Investment Decision under the terms of that agreement (&#147;<B>BBCA MKII</B>&#148;). </P></TD></TR></TABLE>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In order to supply natural gas for the liquefaction process to both FLNG HILLI and FLNG MKII, SESA has entered into
<FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">20-year</FONT> natural gas supply agreements (&#147;<B>GSAs</B>&#148;) with PAE, SUR, PAMPA, and Wintershall. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">With
respect to FLNG HILLI, SESA has filed its request for approval to join the Large Investment Incentive Regime (<I>R&eacute;gimen de Incentivo para Grandes Inversiones</I>, &#147;<B>RIGI</B>&#148;), and has a certificate of authorization for the free
export of LNG for a <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">30-year</FONT> term issued by the Argentine Secretariat of Energy, and an environmental impact assessment approval from the Province of R&iacute;o Negro. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">For both FLNGs to operate year-round, SESA contemplates constructing a dedicated gas pipeline from Vaca Muerta to the San Mat&iacute;as Gulf. Production from
FLNG HILLI is expected to begin in late 2027 or early 2028, while operations of FLNG MKII are expected to start in late 2028. </P>
