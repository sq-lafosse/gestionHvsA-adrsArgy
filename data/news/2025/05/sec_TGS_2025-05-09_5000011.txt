<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><B>Climate Event &#8211;&nbsp;Cerri Complex</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>On March 7, 2025, unprecedented heavy rainfall&#8212;the most extreme in the last 100 years&#8212;was recorded in the city of Bah&#237;a Blanca and adjacent areas, causing flooding across all urban and surrounding regions (the &quot;Event&quot;).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>The Event led to the overflow of the Saladillo Garc&#237;a stream, which flooded the Cerri Complex, halting liquid production and partially affecting natural gas transportation services.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>The natural gas transportation service was gradually restored by March 24, 2025, without having a significant impact on revenues for the Natural Gas Transportation business segment.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Regarding the Liquids Production and Commercialization segment, operations were suspended between March 7, 2025, to mid-April. As of today, we continue prioritizing the safety of operations, and the reactivation of natural gas processing and the production of liquids have begun gradually and partially. This procedure follows the infrastructure set up, starting with the stabilization of external electricity supply, which has been fully resolved. Currently, <B>tgs</B> continues the recovery efforts of the damaged equipment and is focused on fully restoring the plant operations in the short term to reach optimal product stock levels.</P>
