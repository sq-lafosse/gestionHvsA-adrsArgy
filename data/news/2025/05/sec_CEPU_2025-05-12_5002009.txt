<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0 6pt 70.8pt; text-align: justify; text-indent: -70.8pt">&nbsp;<IMG SRC="cepupr1q256k_003.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Buenos
Aires, <B>May 12<SUP>th</SUP></B>, Central Puerto S.A (&ldquo;Central Puerto&rdquo; or the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;) (NYSE: CEPU), the largest
private sector power generation companies in Argentina, reports its consolidated financial results for the <B>First Quarter 2025 (</B>&ldquo;1Q25&rdquo;),
ended on March 31st, 2025.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">A conference call to discuss the 1<B>Q25
results </B>will be held on <B>May 12<SUP>th</SUP>, 2025, at 12:00 PM ET Eastern Time</B> (see details below). All information provided
is presented on a consolidated basis, unless otherwise stated.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Financial statements as of <B>March 31st,
20254, </B>include the effects of the inflation adjustment, applying IAS 29. Accordingly, the financial statements have been stated in
terms of the measuring unit current at the end of the reporting period, including the corresponding financial figures for previous periods
reported for comparative purposes. Growth comparisons refer to the same periods of the previous year, measured in the current unit at
the end of the period, unless otherwise stated. Consequently, the information included in the Financial Statements for the first quarter
ended on <B>March 31st, 2025, </B>is not comparable to the Financial Statements previously published by the company. However, we presented
some figures converted from Argentine Pesos to U.S. dollars for comparison purposes only. The exchange rate used to convert Argentine
Pesos to U.S. dollars was the reference exchange rate (Communication &ldquo;A&rdquo; 3500) reported by the Central Bank for U.S. dollars
for the end of each period. The information presented in U.S. dollars is for the convenience of the reader only and may defer if such
conversion for each period is performed at the exchange rate applicable at the end of the latest period. You should not consider these
translations to be representations that the Argentine Peso amounts actually represent these U.S. dollars amounts or could be converted
into U.S. dollars at the rate indicated.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Definitions and terms used herein are provided
in the Glossary at the end of this document. This release does not contain all the Company&rsquo;s financial information. As a result,
investors should read this release in conjunction with Central Puerto&rsquo;s consolidated financial statements as of and for the fiscal
period ended on <B>March 31st, 2025, </B>and the notes thereto, which will be available on the Company&rsquo;s website.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify; color: #0F2B89"><B>A. Regulatory Updates and
News</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify"><B><BR>
Resolution SE N&deg;603/2024</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">On <B>December 31st, 2024</B>, the Secretariat
of Energy published Resolution N&deg;603/2024 in the Official Gazette. This resolution updated the remuneration values for power and energy
generation of units not committed in contracts. It replaced Annexes I to V of Resolution N&deg;387/2024 and established a 4% increase
in remuneration values effective from January 1st, 2025.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>Resolution SE N&deg;21/2025</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">On <B>January 28th, 2025</B>, the Secretariat
of Energy published Resolution N&deg;21 in the Official Gazette, which establishes the following main aspects:</P>

<UL STYLE="list-style-type: disc">

<LI STYLE="line-height: 115%; text-align: justify; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>New Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs):</B> new generation facilities,
regardless of their&nbsp;technology, are allowed to sign PPAs either with industrial/commercial clients or distribution companies. This
decision lifts the restriction imposed many years ago by clause 9 of Resolution 95/2013. This change applies for power plants with COD
January 1st, 2025 onwards.</LI>

</UL>

<UL STYLE="list-style-type: disc">

<LI STYLE="line-height: 115%; text-align: justify; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Fuel management: </B>since March 1, 2025, thermal generators
are now allowed to manage their own fuel, regardless of which one they use. This decision abolishes Resolution 354/2020, issued in December
2020, and also lifts a restriction imposed by clause 8 of Resolution 95/2013.</LI>

</UL>

<UL STYLE="list-style-type: disc">

<LI STYLE="line-height: 115%; text-align: justify; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>Non-Delivered Energy&nbsp;Costs:</B> As of February 1, 2025,
a set of Non-Delivered Energy&nbsp;Costs has been established: i) USD/MWh 350 up to 5% of the demand,&nbsp;ii) USD/MWh 750 up to 10% of
the demand and&nbsp;iii) USD/MWh 1,500 for more than 10% of the demand. These figures do not represent actual costs to be borne by anyone
but serve as price signals for scarcity. If a shortage in the supply is discovered during the dispatch projections for future years these
&ldquo;costs&rdquo; will be included to act as a proxy of operating costs to better reflect the real cost of generation. This seeks to
give a signal for investment needs.</LI>

</UL>

<UL STYLE="list-style-type: disc">

<LI STYLE="line-height: 115%; text-align: justify; margin: 0pt 0 12pt"><B>&ldquo;Energia Plus&rdquo; framework</B><FONT STYLE="font-family: Times New Roman, Times, Serif">:
</FONT>an ending is settled for the Energ&iacute;a Plus framework. Current contracts will be in place and continue until their ending
date, but new agreements and extensions&nbsp;will have a deadline: October 31st, 2025.</LI>

</UL>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Nuclear generation mainly decreased by the

River. As it was previously stated, Paraguay historically consumed a smaller portion of the energy produced at Yacyret&aacute;: while
this country took only 15% of the generated energy in 2023, by mid-2024 it started to take its full 50% share, leaving Argentina with
a smaller portion of the generated energy.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>EBITDA &amp; ADJUSTED EBITDA </B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">In this release, <B>EBITDA</B>, a non-IFRS financial
measure, is defined as net income for the period, <I>plus</I> finance expenses, <I>minus</I> finance income, <I>minus </I>share of the
profit (loss) of associates, <I>plus (minus) </I>losses (gains) on net monetary position, <I>plus</I> income tax expense, <I>plus</I>
depreciation and amortization, <I>minus</I> net results of discontinued operations.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify"><B>Adjusted EBITDA</B> refers to EBITDA <I>excluding
</I>impairment on property, plant &amp; equipment, foreign exchange difference and interests related to FONI trade receivables and variations
in fair value of biological asset.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Adjusted EBITDA is believed to provide useful
supplemental information to investors about the Company and its results. Adjusted EBITDA is among the measures used by the Company&rsquo;s
management team to evaluate the financial and operating performance and make day-to-day financial and operating decisions. In addition,
Adjusted EBITDA is frequently used by securities analysts, investors, and other parties to evaluate companies in the industry. Adjusted
EBITDA is believed to be helpful to investors because it provides additional information about trends in the core operating performance
prior to considering the impact of capital structure, depreciation, amortization, and taxation on the results.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Calibri, Helvetica, Sans-Serif; margin: 6pt 0; text-align: justify">Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation
or as a substitute for other measures of financial performance reported in accordance with IFRS. Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an
analytical tool, including:</P>
