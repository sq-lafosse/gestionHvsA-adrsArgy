<P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Effects of inflation</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt'>On December 3, 2018, Law No. 27,468 was enacted, sanctioned on November 15, 2018 by the National Argentine Congress. Among other measures, this law abolishes Presidential Decree No. 1,269/02 &#8212;amended by Presidential Decree No. 664/2003 &#8212;&nbsp;through which the controlling entities (among them the CNV) had been instructed not to accept inflation adjusted financial statements. On December 26, 2018, the CNV issued Resolution No. 777/2018, by virtue of which it instructs companies that offer their shares to the public to apply the financial statements restatement method to a stable currency in line with the provisions of IAS 29 &#8220;Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies&#8221;.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>In accordance with such standards, the restatement of financial statements was restarted as from July 1, 2018. In line with the restatement method, non-currency assets and liabilities are restated by an overall price index issued by the Argentine Federation of Professional Councils in Economic Sciences (&#8220;Argentine Federation&#8221;) since their acquisition date or last inflation adjustment (March 1, 2003).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Likewise, comparative information included in the financial statements has also been restated, but this fact has not modified the decisions based on the financial information corresponding to those fiscal years.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt'>For further information, see &#8220;Note 4. Significant Accounting Policies &#8211;&nbsp;d) Restatement to constant currency &#8211;&nbsp;Comparative Information&#8221; to the consolidated financial statements as of December 31, 2024.</P>

<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Activities of the Company in 1Q2025 and 1Q2024</B></P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Revenues</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Total revenues for 1Q2025 increased by Ps. 24,832 million compared to 1Q2024, mainly as a result of the increase in revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation, partially offset by the decline in revenues from the Liquids Production and Commercialization and Midstream segments.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Natural Gas Transportation</B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>During 1Q2025 revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation business segment accounted for 47% of <B>tgs</B> total revenues (11% for 1Q2024). Of the total revenues in this business segment, 80% (75% for 1Q2024) corresponded to firm contracted capacity services.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation segment during 1Q2025 reached Ps. 152,035 million (Ps. 32,643 million in 1Q2024). The increase of Ps. 119,392 million is due to the positive impact of transitional tariff increases received during 2024.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>It is worth noting that the operation of this business segment was partially affected by the climatic event that occurred at the Cerri Complex. However, there was no impact on revenues between March 7 and March 24, 2025. For further details, refer to Note 24 &#8211;&nbsp;Climatic Event at the General Cerri Complex in the condensed interim consolidated financial statements.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Liquids Production and Commercialization </B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>During 1Q2025, revenues for this business segment represented 34% of total revenues (67% for 1Q2024).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Revenues from this business segment amounted to Ps. 111,070 million in 1Q2025 (Ps. 88,753 million lower than in 1Q2024). The negative effect was mainly due to the decrease in: (i) dispatched tons, (ii) the real exchange rate, and (iii) the price of ethane. These effects were partially offset by the positive variation in international reference prices.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Total volumes dispatched recorded a decrease of 27% or 79,970 tons compared to 1Q2024. </P>
<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>6</P>
<HR style='page-break-after:always;border:0;height:3pt;background-color:#909090;margin:8pt 0'><P style=line-height:0;margin:0></P>
<P align=right style='font:11pt Arial;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'><FONT style='font:10pt Times New Roman'><B><IMG src=tgs_5.jpg width=80 height=30 alt='Picture 9' title='Picture 9'></B></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=center style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'><FONT style='font-family:Times New Roman'><IMG src=tgs_7.jpg width=585 height=138 alt='Picture 2' title='Picture 2'></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>The decrease in volumes sold was mainly due to the impact of the climatic event that occurred on March 7, 2025, when unprecedented heavy rainfall caused the overflow of the Saladillo Garc&#237;a stream, which flooded the Cerri Complex and consequently halted the production of liquids and partially affected the natural gas transportation service.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Regarding the Liquids Production and Commercialization business segment, at the Cerri Complex, liquids production was interrupted from March 7, 2025, until mid-April. At that time, prioritizing operational safety, the reactivation of production began partially and gradually. This process was possible as infrastructure issues were resolved.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>For more information see &#8220;Note 24 - Climatic Event at the General Cerri Complex&quot; in the condensed interim consolidated financial statements.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Midstream </B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Midstream revenues decreased by Ps. 5,807 million in 1Q2025 compared to the same period in 2024. This decrease is due to the negative variation in the real exchange rate, an effect that was partially offset by higher natural gas transportation and conditioning services in Vaca Muerta.</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Net cost of sales and administrative and selling expenses</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Net cost of sales, administrative and selling expenses corresponding to 1Q2025 decreased by Ps. 27,054 million. This variation is mainly due to the decrease in the cost of natural gas processed at the Cerri Complex (primarily due to a decrease in price, measured in constant Argentine pesos). This effect was partially offset by the increase in repair and maintenance expenses and depreciations.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:11pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-24.75pt;color:#0070C0'><B>9. Outlook</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>The future of natural gas as an essential fuel for the country's energy matrix is consolidated year after year, which will make Argentina a sustainable country with high growth. We have consolidated ourselves as an integrated service provider in the hydrocarbon industry.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Our strategy aims to position ourselves in a leading role, carrying out vital undertakings for the future of the country, thus consolidating the growth strategy with a leap of magnitude. With this objective, we are adopting an innovative vision, seeking new business opportunities for our clients with a focus on Vaca Muerta.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Continuing our growth plan in the area, we expect to keep developing business opportunities that allow us to generate value for producers in the Vaca Muerta area and enhance Argentina's energy development.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>In the Liquids Production and Commercialization Segment, the strategy will be aimed at optimizing the production mix that allows prioritizing those products and distribution channels that provide higher margins, and to maximizing access to the RTP at reasonable costs. For this, it will be very important to be efficient in the management of our assets, ensuring a coordinated, safe and efficient operation.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>In financial terms, and given the aforementioned facts, we will continue to prudently manage our funds in order to preserve our shareholders&#180; value given the volatile macroeconomic context in which our activities will be carried out. </P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>As regards their daily operations, tgs will remain committed to continuous improvement of each of its processes to optimize the use of the resources and to reduce operating costs. To this end, the Company will carry out actions aimed at the reduction of cost without affecting the reliability and availability of the pipeline system. We will continue with the implementation of various actions, such as the standardization and systematization of risk management in pipelines, compressor stations and processing facilities. Finally, we will deepen training initiatives for the staff for technical and management training resources.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin:0;margin-right:28.3pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Business Overview</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin:0;text-indent:14.2pt;margin-right:28.3pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (&#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) is one of the companies created as a result of the privatization of Gas del Estado S.E. (&#8220;GdE&#8221;). <B>tgs</B> commenced operations on December 28, 1992 and it is mainly engaged in the Transportation of Natural Gas, and Production and Commercialization of natural gas Liquids (&#8220;Liquids&#8221;). TGS&#8217;s pipeline system connects major natural gas fields in southern and western Argentina with natural gas distributors and industries in those areas and in the greater Buenos Aires area. The natural gas transportation license to operate this system was exclusively granted to <B>tgs</B> for a period of thirty-five years (&#8220;the License&#8221;). <B>tgs</B> is entitled to a one-time extension of ten years provided that it has essentially met the obligations imposed by the License and by <I>Ente Nacional Regulador del Gas</I> (National Gas Regulatory Body or &#8220;ENARGAS&#8221;). The General Cerri Gas Processing Complex (the &#8220;Cerri Complex&#8221;), where TGS processes natural gas to obtain liquids, was transferred from GdE along with the gas transmission assets. <B>tgs</B> also provides midstream services, which mainly consist of gas treatment, removal of impurities from the natural gas stream, gas compression, wellhead gas gathering and pipeline construction, operation and maintenance services. In addition, telecommunications services are provided through the subsidiary Telcosur S.A. (&#8220;Telcosur&#8221;). These services consist of data transmission services through a network of terrestrial and digital radio relay.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>Subsequently, the corporate purpose of the Company was modified to incorporate the development of complementary activities, incidental, linked and / or derived from natural gas transportation, such as the generation and commercialization of electric power and the provision of other services for the hydrocarbon sector in general.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>

<P align=justify style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>On March 7, 2025, unprecedented heavy rainfall&#8212;the most extreme in the last 100 years&#8212;was recorded in the city of Bah&#237;a Blanca and adjacent areas, causing flooding across all urban and surrounding regions (the &quot;Event&quot;).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>The Event led to the overflow of the Saladillo Garc&#237;a stream, which flooded the Cerri Complex, halting liquid production and partially affecting natural gas transportation services. It is also worth noting that the external electrical distribution system, as well as the Complex's electrical generation and distribution facilities, were affected.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>The natural gas transportation service was gradually restored by March 24, 2025, without having a significant impact on revenues for the Natural Gas Transportation business segment.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>Regarding the Liquids Production and Commercialization segment, operations were suspended between March 7, 2025, to mid-April. As of today, we continue prioritizing the safety of operations, and the reactivation of processing and the production of liquids has begun gradually and partially. This procedure follows the infrastructure set up, starting with the stabilization of external electricity supply, which has been fully resolved. Currently, tgs continues the recovery efforts of the damaged equipment and is focused on fully restoring the plant operations in the short term to reach optimal product stock levels.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>The Company is currently evaluating the full impact of these events. In the three-month period ended March 31, 2025, the Company recorded a loss of Ps. 14,058,432, corresponding to expenses related to the event and impairment charges for materials and other property, plant, and equipment (the latter amounting to Ps. 10,903,773). At this stage, the final cost of the event has not yet been determined. For reference, the average standard daily liquids production from January to March amounts to approximately 3,400 tons.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>It is worth noting, that the Company has coverage for property damage and business interruption, which is subject to the terms and conditions of insurance policies and applicable sublimits. The property damage deductible amounts to US$1 million, while the business interruption coverage includes a 60-day waiting period for the Liquids Production and Commercialization segment. The Company has initiated negotiations with insurers, and is still in the preliminary stages, and thus, it is premature to determine the estimated recovery amount and collection date.</P>
