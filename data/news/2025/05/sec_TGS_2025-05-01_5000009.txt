<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt;text-indent:0.25pt'>Dear Sirs,</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>I am writing to you in my capacity as Head of Market Relations of <B>Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A.</B> (&#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221;), to report that the Annual General, Special and Class Shareholders&#8217; Meeting held on this date approved: </P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt;color:#000000'>(1) To reduce <B>tgs</B>&#8217; capital due to cancellation of <B>tgs</B>&#8217; treasury shares for an amount of AR$ 41,734,225 representing 41,734,225 Class B book-entry shares of a nominal value of AR$ 1 each and entitled to 1 vote per share. As a result of said capital reduction, <B>tgs</B>&#8217; capital stock shall turn from AR$ 794,495,283 to AR$ 752,761,058, and</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>(2) To amend sections 5 and 6 and eliminate section 13 of <B>tgs</B>&#8217; Bylaws in order to:</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>- Establish that Class A shares shall represent 51% or more of the capital stock, while Class B shares shall represent the remaining capital stock.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt'>- Eliminate from <B>tgs</B>&#8217; Bylaws any reference to Class C shares as there no longer exist any Class C shares.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:11pt Book Antiqua;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:6pt;color:#000000'>- Include the possibility to convert Class A shares into Class B shares, as long as Class A shares continue to represent at least 51% of the capital stock after conversion. </P>
