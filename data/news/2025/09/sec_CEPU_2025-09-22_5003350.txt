<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0 198.45pt"><B>Ref.: CEPU-ECOGAS Corporate Reorganization. Relevant Fact.</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0 0 0 198.45pt"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0">From my consideration:</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">I am hereby writing to you, in my capacity as Head
of Market Relations of <B>Central Puerto S.A.</B> (hereinafter, the &quot;<U>Company</U>&quot; or &quot;<U>CEPU&quot;),</U> in relation
to the Split-off-merger operation (the &quot;<U>Split-off-Merger</U>&quot;) between the Company and ECOGAS Inversiones S.A. (&quot;<U>ECOGAS&quot;),</U>
to inform the Shareholders that, after obtaining due authorizations from the National Securities Commission (the &quot;<U>CNV</U>&quot;)
and the Buenos Aires Stock Exchange (&quot;<U>BCBA&quot;)</U>, in relation to the Split-off-merger operation (the &quot;<U>Split-Off-Merger</U>&quot;)
between the Company and ECOGAS Inversiones S.A. (&quot;<U>ECOGAS&quot;),</U> as authorized by the CEPU and ECOGAS Extraordinary General
Shareholders' Meetings of May 22, 2025, the Company will proceed with the Split-Off on October 1, 2025.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">For this reason, the investing public is informed
that, in accordance with the terms of the definitive corporate reorganization agreement signed on June 17, 2025 between the Company and
ECOGAS (the &quot;<B>Definitive Corporate Reorganization Agreement</B>&quot;), the following events will take place on October 1, 2025,
among others:</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0in">1)<FONT STYLE="font-size: 7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
</FONT>ECOGAS will receive the entirety of the Split-Off CEPU Equity, consisting of: (a) 59,986,580 Class &quot;A&quot; book-entry ordinary
shares, with a par value of $1 each and with the right to one vote per share of ECOGAS; (b) 33,369 non-endorsable registered ordinary
shares, of $1 par value each and entitled to one vote per share of Energ&iacute;a Sudamericana S.A.; (c) 27,597,032 Class &quot;B&quot;
book-entry ordinary shares, with a par value of $1 each and entitled to one vote per share of Distribuidora de Gas del Centro S.A.; and
(d) the sum of $305,000,000 in cash;</P>
<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0in"></P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0in">2)<FONT STYLE="font-size: 7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
</FONT>All Class &quot;A&quot; shares of ECOGAS (59,986,580 shares with a par value of $1 each and with the right to one vote per share)
will be cancelled;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0in">3)<FONT STYLE="font-size: 7pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
</FONT>80,973,264 new Class &quot;D&quot; shares of ECOGAS will be issued, with a par value of $1 each and with the right to one vote
per share, to be distributed among CEPU shareholders based on their shareholdings in said company according to the register of Caja de
Valores S.A. as of the date of registration, which will be September 26, 2025 (the &quot;<B>Registration </B>Date&quot;).&quot;), and
to the Exchange Ratio determined in one Class &quot;D&quot; share of ECOGAS for every 18.6694 shares held in CEPU. Fractions of shares
will not be delivered.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">As indicated in the Corporate Reorganization
Agreement, the new shares of ECOGAS to be delivered to those shareholders of CEPU who hold their interests in such company through CEPU'<I>s
American Depositary Receipts</I> program (&quot;<B>CEPU ADRs</B>&quot;) as of the Record Date, will be delivered to JPMorgan Chase Bank,
N.A. (&quot;<B>JPMorgan</B>&quot;) in its capacity as depositary of the CEPU ADR program and, if they are not capable of being delivered
in the form of certificates representing such New ECOGAS Shares, they will be retained by JPMorgan, in its capacity as depositary of the
CEPU ADR program, which will dispose of them, in accordance with the provisions of the <I>Amended and Restated Deposit Agreement</I> dated
October 16, 2023 between CEPU, JPMorgan, and CEPU ADR holders, and any agreements or instructions JPMorgan enters into with, or receives
from, CEPU.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">In this regard, ECOGAS is in the process of creating
a program of <I>Global Depositary Receipts</I> of ECOGAS (the &quot;<B>GDR Program</B>&quot;), which will represent <I>Global Depositary
Shares</I> (&quot;<B>GDSs</B>&quot;) whose assets will have as an underlying part of the new shares of ECOGAS, in order to offer investors
who have their shares in CEPU through CEPU ADRs the possibility of receiving securities representing the New Shares abroad and grant them
a reasonable period of approximately 90 days from the date of the Restricted Deposit Agreement to carry out operations aimed at receiving
their shares in the local market, at their discretion.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Given that the Split-Off-Merger is an operation
exempt from registration with the U.S. Securities Exchange Commission (&quot;<B>SEC&quot;),</B> the Program must be restricted, and may
only be accessed by investors who have their shares in CEPU through ADRs as of the registration date determined by JPMorgan and who submit
the certifications required pursuant to Regulation S and Rule 144 A of the Securities Act of the United States of America. 1933 (<I>Securities
Act</I>) and investors who, after the initial distribution, acquire GDRs issued as a result of the Split-Off-Merger through the trading
mechanisms and with the applicable restrictions.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Consequently, the GDRs will be delivered to
those holders of CEPU ADRs that are eligible under the standards mentioned, to the extent that they have complied with the certifications
and other documentation required by JPMorgan, in the times and forms that said entity provided accordingly.</P>
<P STYLE="font: 12pt/115% Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"></P>
