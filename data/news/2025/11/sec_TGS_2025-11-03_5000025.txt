<DIV style=margin-left:85.05pt;width:446.55pt><P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:-14.2pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with a firm-contracted capacity of 89.7 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d. We are one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, our infrastructure investment in the Vaca Muerta formation places us as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina.</I></P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:-14.2pt'><I>Our shares are traded on NYSE (New York Stock Exchange) and BYMA (Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.).</I></P>

(GIP), led by the Sielecki family, and PCT L.L.C. hold the remaining 50%.</I></P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'><B>tgs</B> reported comprehensive income for the 3Q2025 of Ps. 112,059 million, equivalent to Ps. 148.86 per share (Ps. 744.32 per ADS), compared to income of Ps. 68,802 million, or Ps. 91.40 per share (Ps. 457.00 per ADS) for the three-month period ended September 30, 2024 (3Q2024).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Arial;margin:0'><FONT style='font:9pt Verdana'> </FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Arial;margin:0;margin-right:-28.35pt'><FONT style='font-family:Times New Roman'><IMG src=tgs_4.jpg width=330 height=195 alt='Picture 2' title='Picture 2'></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Arial;margin:0;margin-right:-28.35pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>Operating profit for the 3Q2025 totaled Ps. 172,026 million, an increase of Ps. 33,567 million compared to 3Q2024. This variation was mainly driven by higher revenues from the Liquids Production and Commercialization and Midstream segments, which rose by Ps. 70,423 million and Ps. 24,851 million, respectively.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>On the other hand, revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation segment decreased by Ps. 6,682 million, partially offsetting the positive effects mentioned above.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>Net cost of sales and administrative and selling expenses increased by Ps. 45,571 million, while other operating results declined by Ps. 9,453 million.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-21.25pt;color:#000000'>Financial results grew by Ps. 31,087 million.</P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><B>Highlights during 3Q2025 and beyond</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>Following the national and international public tender organized by Energ&#237;a Argentina S.A. (&#8220;ENARSA&#8221;) to expand the Perito Moreno Pipeline (GPM) &#8212; aimed at increasing natural gas transportation capacity at Vaca Muerta by 14 MMm&#179;/d &#8212;, <B>tgs</B> was awarded the execution of the project on October 17, 2025. This initiative involves an estimated investment of US$ 560 million and has been declared of public interest, as it supports the growth of natural gas transportation capacity from Vaca Muerta.&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>Additionally, <B>tgs</B> will execute an investment of approximately US$ 220 million to expand the transportation capacity of natural gas by 12 MMm&#179;/d in the final sections of the pipeline system operated within the Natural Gas Transportation segment.&nbsp;</P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Net cost of sales and administrative and selling expenses</B></FONT> increased by Ps. 45,571 million, mainly due to higher: (i) cost of natural gas purchased for liquids production &#8212; primarily driven by increased consumption and, to a lesser extent, by the inflation-adjusted price under IAS 29 &#8212; amounting to Ps. 28,470 million; (ii) depreciation charges of Ps. 7,804 million; (iii) repair and maintenance expenses of Ps. 6,311 million; and, (iv) taxes, fees and contributions of Ps. 5,090 million, mainly related to higher export duties. These effects were partially compensated by lower labor costs amounting to Ps. 2,132 million.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Financial results</B></FONT> are presented on a nominal basis, with the impact in the monetary position shown as a single line item. In 3Q2025, negative financial results decreased by Ps. 31,087 million compared to 3Q2024. This improvement was mainly driven by higher returns on financial assets totaling Ps. 43,406 million and a lower loss on monetary position of Ps. 10,726 million. These effects were partially offset by a higher negative foreign exchange difference of Ps. 21,759 million.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Other net operating results</B></FONT> reported a loss of Ps. 8,870 million in 3Q2025, compared to a gain of Ps. 583 million in 3Q2024. The negative variation was attributable to a Ps. 10,248 million loss related to expenses and impairment of materials and other PPE items associated with the weather event that occurred on March 7, 2025, at the Cerri Complex. This impact was partially offset by insurance recoveries amounting to Ps. 1,292 million.</P>
