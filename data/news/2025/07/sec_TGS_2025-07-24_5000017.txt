<P style="margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:7.333px; text-indent:0.333px; font-family:'Book Antiqua,Times New Roman'; font-size:11pt" align=justify>Dear Sirs,</P>
<P style="margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:7.333px; font-family:'Book Antiqua,Times New Roman'; font-size:11pt" align=justify>I am writing to you in my capacity as Alternate Head of Market Relations of Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (<font style="font-family:'Arial Unicode MS,Times New Roman'">&#8220;</font><B>tgs</B><font style="font-family:'Arial Unicode MS,Times New Roman'">&#8221;</font>), to report that on this date, Decree no. 495/2025 (Decree no. 495) was published in the Argentine Official Gazette. By means of said Decree, the Executive Branch ratified the Memorandum of Agreement for the Extension of the License granted to <B>tgs</B> for the rendering of the Gas Transportation Public Service, which was entered into between <B>tgs</B> and the Ministry of Economy on July 11, 2025 (the <font style="font-family:'Arial Unicode MS,Times New Roman'">&#8220;</font>MOA<font style="font-family:'Arial Unicode MS,Times New Roman'">&#8221;</font>).</P>
<P style="margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:7.333px; font-family:'Book Antiqua,Times New Roman'; font-size:11pt" align=justify>Decree no. 495 established the extension of the License granted to <B>tgs</B> by means of Decree no. 2458/1992, for an additional period of twenty (20) years as from December 28, 2027, pursuant to the terms of the MOA that was ratified.</P>
<P style="margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:7.333px; font-family:'Book Antiqua,Times New Roman'; font-size:11pt" align=justify>Additionally, within ten (10) business days as from publication of Decree 495, <B>tgs</B> must submit all documentation that proves withdrawal and waiving of its claims, remedies and actions -administrative, arbitral or judicial- current or in the course of execution, in Argentina or abroad <font style="font-family:'Arial Unicode MS,Times New Roman'">&#8211;</font> pursued by <B>tgs</B> or its controlling shareholder against the National Government, pursuant to the terms of the MOA.</P>
