Relevante&#148;) of April&nbsp;23, 2024 and our Financial Statements, Plaintiffs in the proceedings brought by Petersen Energ&iacute;a Inversora S.A.U., Petersen Energ&iacute;a S.A.U., Eton Park Capital Management, L.P., Eton Park Master Fund, LTD.
and Eton Park Fund, L.P. (collectively, &#147;Plaintiffs&#148;) against the Argentine Republic (the &#147;Republic&#148;) and YPF S.A. (&#147;YPF&#148;), had filed a turnover motion against the Republic, in connection with the enforcement of the
judgment entered by the District Court in New York on September&nbsp;15, 2023, asking the District Court to require the Republic to turnover to Plaintiffs its Class&nbsp;D shares of YPF. On June&nbsp;30, 2025, the District Court granted
Plaintiffs&#146; turnover motion, ordering the Republic to (i)&nbsp;transfer its Class&nbsp;D shares of YPF to a global custody account at the Bank of New York Mellon (&#147;BNYM&#148;) in New York within 14 days of the date of the order; and
(ii)&nbsp;instruct BNYM to initiate a transfer of the Republic&#146;s ownership interests in its Class&nbsp;D shares of YPF to Plaintiffs or their designees within one business day of the date on which the shares are deposited into the account. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">In addition, on the same date, in the proceedings brought by Bainbridge Fund Ltd. against the Republic, the District Court partially granted Bainbridge&#146;s
turnover motion, ordering the Republic to (i)&nbsp;transfer its Class&nbsp;A and Class&nbsp;D shares of YPF to a global custody account at BNYM in New York within 14 days from the date of the order; and (ii)&nbsp;instruct BNYM to initiate a transfer
of the Republic&acute;s ownership interest in its Class&nbsp;A and Class&nbsp;D shares of YPF to Bainbridge or its designees within one business day of the date on which the shares are deposited into the account. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">YPF is not a party to either of the turnover proceedings referenced above. </P>
<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The District Court&#146;s decisions on the turnover motions may be appealed by the Republic in accordance with applicable procedural rules. </P>
