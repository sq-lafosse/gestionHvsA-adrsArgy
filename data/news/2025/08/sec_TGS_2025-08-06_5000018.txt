<DIV style=margin-left:85.05pt;width:446.55pt><P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:-14.2pt'><I>Transportadora de Gas del Sur (&quot;</I><B><I>tgs</I></B><I>&quot;, &quot;the Company&quot;, &#8220;us&#8221;, &#8220;our&#8221;, or &#8220;we&#8221;) is the leader in Argentina in the transportation of natural gas, transporting approximately 60% of the gas consumed in the country, through more than 5,700 miles of gas pipelines, with a firm-contracted capacity of 89.1 MMm<SUP>3</SUP>/d. We are one of the main natural gas processors. In addition, our infrastructure investment in the Vaca Muerta formation places us as one of the main Midstreamers in Argentina.</I></P>
<P align=justify style='font:7pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:-14.2pt'><I>Our shares are traded on NYSE (New York Stock Exchange) and BYMA (Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.).</I></P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><B>Highlights during 2Q2025 and beyond</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>Following two months of intensive recovery efforts, the Company successfully restored full operations at the Cerri Complex during the first week of May, after the unprecedented weather event that occurred on March 7, 2025, in Bah&#237;a Blanca and surrounding areas. Thanks to the commitment of our teams and the coordinated work of more than 150 suppliers and contractors, we were able to overcome the damage caused by the flooding and resume natural gas liquids processing under the highest standards of safety and efficiency.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>On May 22, 2025, Energ&#237;a Argentina S.A. (&#8220;ENARSA&#8221;) launched a national and international public tender for the expansion of the Perito Moreno Gas Pipeline (&#8220;GPM&#8221;), aiming to increase the natural gas transportation capacity at Vaca Muerta in 14 MMm&#179;/d. The submission of bids, in which <B>tgs</B> was the only bidder, took place on July 28, 2025, while the tender winner is scheduled to be awarded on October 13, 2025. Works are expected to begin thereafter and would take approximately 18 months to be completed. &nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>At the Board of Directors meeting held on May 28, 2025, a cash dividend was approved totaling Ps. 202,704 million (equivalent to Ps. 269.28 per share), following the approval by the Ordinary, Extraordinary, and Special General Shareholders' Meeting of April 30, 2025.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>Under Decree No. 371/2025, the Secretariat of Energy was appointed as the enforcement authority responsible for implementing contractual and tariff modifications. Accordingly, on June 4, 2025, Resolution No. 241/2025 was issued, establishing a monthly update mechanism for transportation tariffs (the &#8220;Periodic Adjustment&#8221;), replacing the previous semiannual scheme. This new methodology is based on a composite adjustment equally weighted between the monthly variation of the Consumer Price Index (&#8220;CPI&#8221;) and the Wholesale Domestic Price Index (&#8220;WPI&#8221;).&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Wingdings;margin-left:-18pt'>&#216;</KBD>On July 24, 2025, Decree No. 495/2025 was published in the Official Gazette, through which the National Executive Branch granted an extension of the license awarded to <B>tgs</B> under Decree No. 2458/1992. The extension covers an additional 20-year period starting on December 28, 2027.&nbsp;</P>
