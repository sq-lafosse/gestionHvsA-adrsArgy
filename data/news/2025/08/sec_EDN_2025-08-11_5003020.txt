<P STYLE="font: 16pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: center; text-indent: 7.1pt; color: #1F497D"><B>PRESS RELEASE</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>EDENOR REPORTS AN EBITDA OF $ 289,385 MILLION</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 14pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>MAIN FINANCIAL INDICATORS</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman,serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><IMG SRC="edn202508086k2_002.jpg" ALT=""></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: center"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">*The figures for the six-month period of the previous year have
been restated to reflect the changes in the purchasing power of the Argentine peso, in accordance with International Accounting Standard
No. 29 and the provisions of General Resolution No. 777/2018 of the National Securities Commission.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B>Buenos Aires, August 8, 2025</B>, the Board of Directors of
Empresa Distribuidora y Comercializadora Norte S.A. (&#8220;Edenor&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) approved today its financial statements
for the period ended June 30, 2025, which show a profit of ARS 131,004 million.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 8pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">Taking into consideration the impact of the implemented electricity
rate adjustments, the results of operations for the period reflect an operating and financial improvement, which allowed the Company to
continue making the investments necessary for maintaining and improving the quality of the service it provides, with technology and innovations,
promoting responsible and efficient use of energy. This is reflected in the levels reached by our SAIDI and SAIFI indicators, as well
as in the satisfaction levels of our customers.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">In the first half of 2025, compared to the same period
of the previous year, both <B>revenue</B> and the <B>distribution margin </B>reflect an improvement with increases of 22% and 6%, respectively.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">The <B>EBITDA</B> resulted in a profit of ARS 289,385 million,
as a consequence of the higher revenue recorded due to the restoring of the electricity rate. Without taking into consideration the results
deriving from the Agreement on the Regularization of Payment Obligations, the <B>EBITDA </B>would result in a profit of ARS 121,165</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman,serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">The <B>profit for the period</B> as of June 30, 2025 amounted to
ARS 131,004 million, also showing operating cost optimization.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0 0 0 3.3pt; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 11pt Arial,sans-serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify">As for <B>Investments</B>, in the first half of 2025, they amounted
to ARS 163,538 million, which demonstrates Edenor&#8217;s commitment to improving service quality and expanding the service.</P>
