<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'>The following discussion of the financial condition and results of operations of Transportadora de Gas del Sur S.A. (&#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221; or &#8220;the Company&#8221;) should be read in conjunction with the Company's Consolidated Financial Statements as of June 30, 2025 and December 31, 2024, and for the six-month periods ended June 30, 2025 and 2024. <P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt'>The condensed interim consolidated financial statements of the Company for the six-month periods ended June 30, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022 and 2021 have been subject to a limited review performed jointly by Price Waterhouse &amp; Co. S.R.L. and Pistrelli, Henry Martin and Asociados S.A. </P>
<P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Effects of inflation</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt'>On December 3, 2018, Law No. 27,468 was enacted, sanctioned on November 15, 2018 by the National Argentine Congress. Among other measures, this law abolishes Presidential Decree No. 1,269/02 &#8212;amended by Presidential Decree No. 664/2003 &#8212;&nbsp;through which the controlling entities (among them the CNV) had been instructed not to accept inflation adjusted financial statements. On December 26, 2018, the CNV issued Resolution No. 777/2018, by virtue of which it instructs companies that offer their shares to the public to apply the financial statements restatement method to a stable currency in line with the provisions of IAS 29 &#8220;Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies&#8221;.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>In accordance with such standards, the restatement of financial statements was restarted as from July 1, 2018. In line with the restatement method, non-currency assets and liabilities are restated by an overall price index issued by the Argentine Federation of Professional Councils in Economic Sciences (&#8220;Argentine Federation&#8221;) since their acquisition date or last inflation adjustment (March 1, 2003).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Likewise, comparative information included in the financial statements has also been restated, but this fact has not modified the decisions based on the financial information corresponding to those fiscal years.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt'>For further information, see &#8220;Note 4. Significant Accounting Policies &#8211;&nbsp;d) Restatement to constant currency &#8211;&nbsp;Comparative Information&#8221; to the consolidated financial statements as of December 31, 2024.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:3.6pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P style='font:10pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-left:72pt;color:#0070C0'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:10pt Verdana;margin-left:-18pt'><B>1.</B></KBD><B>Analysis of consolidated results of operations</B>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>The following table presents a summary of the consolidated results of operations for the six-month periods ended June 30, 2025 (&#8220;6M2025&#8221;) and June 30, 2024 (&#8220;6M2024&#8221;):</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=center style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'><FONT style='font-family:Times New Roman'><IMG src=tgs_4.jpg width=585 height=195 alt='Picture 1' title='Picture 1'></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Activities of the Company in 6M2025 and 6M2024</B></P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><B>Revenues</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Total revenues for 6M2025 increased by Ps. 4,735 million compared to 6M2024, mainly as a result of the increase in revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation segment, partially offset by the decline in revenues from the Liquids Production and Commercialization and Midstream segments.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#000000'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Natural Gas Transportation</B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>During 6M2025 revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation business segment accounted for 45% of <B>tgs</B> total revenues (29% for 6M2024). Of the total revenues in this business segment, 80% (84% for 6M2024) corresponded to firm contracted capacity services.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>On April 30, 2025, Resolution No. 256/2025 was published, establishing the framework for the Five-Year Tariff Review (&#8220;5YTR&#8221;) for the 2025&#8211;2030 period. It includes an initial tariff increase of 3.67% to be applied in 31 monthly installments, a regulated investment plan, and a new scheme for the periodic adjustment of tariffs.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Subsequently, in line with Decree No. 371/2025, the Secretariat of Energy introduced a monthly tariff update, replacing the previous semiannual scheme. This methodology was formalized by ENARGAS through Resolutions No. 350/2025 and No. 421/2025, which approved adjustments of 2.81% and 0.62%, respectively, also incorporating the increase corresponding to the 5YTR.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Thus, revenues from the Natural Gas Transportation segment during 6M2025 reached Ps. 312,439 million (Ps. 198,562 million in 6M2024). The increase of Ps. 113,877 million is due to the positive impact of transitional tariff increases received during 2024 and 2025 as well as those corresponding to the 5YTR framework.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>It is worth noting that the operation of this business segment was partially affected by the climatic event that occurred at the Cerri Complex. However, there was no impact on revenues between March 7 and March 24, 2025. For further details, refer to Note 24 &#8211;&nbsp;Climatic Event at the General Cerri Complex in the Condensed Interim Consolidated Financial Statements.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>On May 22, 2025, Energ&#237;a Argentina S.A. (&#8220;ENARSA&#8221;) launched a national and international public tender for the expansion of the Perito Moreno Gas Pipeline (&#8220;GPM&#8221;), with the objective of increasing natural gas transportation capacity from Vaca Muerta by 14 MMm&#179;/d. Bids were submitted on July 28, 2025, and the award is scheduled for October 13, 2025. Construction is expected to begin thereafter, with an estimated execution period of 18 months. This project was declared of national public interest </P>

<P align=right style='font:11pt Arial;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'><FONT style='font:10pt Times New Roman'><B><IMG src=tgs_1.jpg width=115 height=44 alt='Picture 9' title='Picture 9'></B></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'><FONT style='font:9pt Verdana'>and falls under the private initiative regime, meaning that the awarded party will be responsible for the design, financing, construction, operation, and maintenance of the facilities.</FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt'>On July 24, 2025, Decree No. 495/2025 was published in the Official Gazette, through which the National Executive Branch ordered the extension of the license granted to <B>tgs</B> by Decree No. 2458/1992, for an additional period of 20 years starting from December 28, 2027.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Liquids Production and Commercialization </B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>During 6M2025, revenues for this business segment represented 34% of total revenues (52% for 6M2024).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Revenues from this business segment amounted to Ps. 238,415 million in 6M2025 (Ps. 119,520 million lower than in 6M2024). The negative effect was mainly due to the decrease in: (i) dispatched tons, (ii) the real exchange rate, and (iii) the international reference prices. These effects were partially offset by the positive variation in the prices of propane and butane sold in the domestic market.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Total volumes dispatched recorded a decrease of 21.9% or 118,490 tons compared to 6M2024. </P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=center style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt'><FONT style='font-family:Times New Roman'><IMG src=tgs_5.jpg width=585 height=138 alt='Picture 2' title='Picture 2'></FONT>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>The decrease in volumes sold was mainly due to the impact of the climatic event that occurred on March 7, 2025, when unprecedented heavy rainfall caused the overflow of the Saladillo Garc&#237;a stream, which flooded the Cerri Complex and consequently halted the production of liquids and partially affected the natural gas transportation service.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Regarding the Liquids Production and Commercialization business segment, at the Cerri Complex, liquids production was interrupted from March 7, 2025, until the end of April. Operations gradually resumed thereafter, reaching normal production levels by early May. This process was made possible as infrastructure issues were resolved.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>For more information see &#8220;Note 24 - Climatic Event at the General Cerri Complex&quot; in the Condensed Interim Consolidated Financial Statements.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'><FONT style='border-bottom:1px solid #0070C0'><B>Midstream </B></FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:10pt;margin-bottom:0pt'>Midstream revenues increased by Ps. 10,379 million in 6M2025 compared to the same period in 2024. <P align=justify style='font:11pt Verdana;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:3pt;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-24.75pt;color:#0070C0'><B>10. Outlook</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:10pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>The future of natural gas as an essential fuel for the country's energy matrix is consolidated year after year, which will make Argentina a sustainable country with high growth. We have consolidated ourselves as an integrated service provider in the hydrocarbon industry.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Our strategy aims to position ourselves in a leading role, carrying out vital undertakings for the future of the country, thus consolidating the growth strategy with a leap of magnitude. With this objective, </P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'><FONT style='font:9pt Verdana'>we are adopting an innovative vision, seeking new business opportunities for our clients with a focus on Vaca Muerta.</FONT></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>Continuing our growth plan in the area, we expect to keep developing business opportunities that allow us to generate value for producers in the Vaca Muerta area and enhance Argentina's energy development.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>In the Liquids Production and Commercialization Segment, the strategy will be aimed at optimizing the production mix that allows prioritizing those products and distribution channels that provide higher margins, and to maximizing access to the RTP at reasonable costs. For this, it will be very important to be efficient in the management of our assets, ensuring a coordinated, safe and efficient operation.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>In financial terms, and given the aforementioned facts, we will continue to prudently manage our funds in order to preserve our shareholders&#180; value given the volatile macroeconomic context in which our activities will be carried out. </P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0'>As regards their daily operations, <B>tgs</B> will remain committed to the continuous improvement of each of its processes in order to optimize resource utilization and thereby reduce operating costs. To this end, the Company aims to maintain the levels of operational reliability that represent a key commitment to its customers. Accordingly, the Company will continue implementing various initiatives, such as the standardization and systematization of risk management across pipelines, compressor plants, and processing facilities. Finally, we will deepen training initiatives for the staff for technical and management training resources. </P>

(&#8220;<B>tgs</B>&#8221; or the &#8220;Company&#8221;) is one of the companies created as a result of the privatization of Gas del Estado S.E. (&#8220;GdE&#8221;). <B>tgs</B> commenced operations on December 28, 1992 and it is mainly engaged in the Transportation of Natural Gas, and Production and Commercialization of natural gas Liquids (&#8220;Liquids&#8221;). TGS&#8217;s pipeline system connects major natural gas fields in southern and western Argentina with natural gas distributors and industries in those areas and in the greater Buenos Aires area. The natural gas transportation license to operate this system was exclusively granted to <B>tgs</B> for a period of thirty-five years (&#8220;the License&#8221;). <B>tgs</B> is entitled to a one-time extension of ten years provided that it has essentially met the obligations imposed by the License and by <I>Ente Nacional Regulador del Gas</I> (National Gas Regulatory Body or &#8220;ENARGAS&#8221;). The General Cerri Gas Processing Complex (the &#8220;Cerri Complex&#8221;), where TGS processes natural gas to obtain liquids, was transferred from GdE along with the gas transmission assets. <B>tgs</B> also provides midstream services, which mainly consist of gas treatment, removal of impurities from the natural gas stream, gas compression, wellhead gas gathering and pipeline construction, operation and maintenance services. In addition, telecommunications services are provided through the subsidiary Telcosur S.A. (&#8220;Telcosur&#8221;). These services consist of data transmission services through a network of terrestrial and digital radio relay.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>The Company operates in a complex economic environment whose main variables have recently had strong volatility as a result of political and economic events at the national level.</P>

<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>The accumulated inflation as of June 30, 2025 was 15.1%, with a monthly deceleration compared to the early months of the year. <P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>On the international front, the Government reached an extended facilities agreement with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) for US$ 20 billion, with a 48-month term. <P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>This phase, announced on April 11, mainly includes: (i) the flexibilization of the exchange rate regime where the dollar exchange rate in the Free Exchange Market (MLC) can fluctuate within a moving band between $1,000 and $1,400, with the limits expanding at a rate of 1% per month, (ii) the elimination of the blend dollar scheme, (iii) the removal of exchange restrictions for individuals, (iv) authorization of the distribution of profits to foreign shareholders starting from the fiscal years beginning in 2025 (i.e., payments allowed from 2026 onward), and (v) the deadlines for the payment of foreign trade operations are relaxed.</P>

<HR style='border:0;height:0;width:0;margin:14pt 0 0 0'><P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:28.35pt;color:#0070C0'><B>17.2</B><FONT style='font-family:Times New Roman;color:#000000'> </FONT><B>Fair value measurement hierarchy and estimates</B></P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt;margin-right:-0.05pt'>According to IFRS Accounting Standard 13, the fair value hierarchy introduces three levels of inputs based on the lowest level of input significant to the overall fair value. These levels are:</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin-top:6pt;margin-bottom:6pt;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 1</B></FONT>: includes financial assets and liabilities whose fair values are estimated using quoted prices (unadjusted) in active markets for identical assets and liabilities. The instruments included in this level primarily include balances in mutual funds and public or private bonds listed on the <I>Bolsas y Mercados Argentinos S.A.</I> (&#8220;BYMA&#8221;). Mutual funds mainly invest in highly liquid instruments with low price risk.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 2</B></FONT>: includes financial assets and liabilities whose fair value is estimated using different assumptions quoted prices included within Level 1 that are observable for the asset or liability, either directly (i.e. as prices) or indirectly (for example, derived from prices). Within this level, the Company includes those derivate financial instruments for which it was not able to find an active market.&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt;color:#0070C0'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:32.2pt'><KBD style='position:absolute;font:9pt Symbol;margin-left:-18pt'><FONT style=color:#0070C0>&#183;</FONT></KBD><FONT style=color:#0070C0><B>Level 3</B></FONT>: includes financial instruments for which the assumptions used in estimating fair value are not based on observable market information.&nbsp;</P>
<P style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:36pt'>&nbsp;</P>

<P align=justify style='font:12pt Times New Roman;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>On March 7, 2025, unprecedented heavy rainfall&#8212;the most extreme in the last 100 years&#8212;was recorded in the city of Bah&#237;a Blanca and adjacent areas, causing flooding across all urban and surrounding regions (the &quot;Event&quot;).</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>The Event led to the overflow of the Saladillo Garc&#237;a stream, which flooded the Cerri Complex, halting liquid production and partially affecting natural gas transportation services. It is also worth noting that the external electrical distribution system, as well as the Complex's electrical generation and distribution facilities, were affected.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>The natural gas transportation service was gradually restored and is currently operating at full capacity, without any significant impact on the revenues of the Natural Gas Transportation business segment.</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>&nbsp;</P>
<P align=justify style='font:9pt Verdana;margin:0;margin-left:14.2pt'>Regarding the Liquids Production and Commercialization segment, at the Cerri Complex, production was completely halted from March 7, 2025, until early May.</P>
